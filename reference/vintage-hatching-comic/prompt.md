## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the author's comment on both the set post and the reel. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns one uploaded photo into a vertical comparison poster:

- **Layout.** One standalone vertical image, 2:3, split 50/50 into two equal horizontal panels separated by a thin white line, with no borders, labels, text overlays, or watermarks.
- **Top half.** The original photograph preserved faithfully — subjects, people, poses, architecture, vehicles, objects, composition, perspective, natural lighting, colors, sky and clouds — with no stylization, retouching, distortion, or unnecessary cropping.
- **Bottom half — transformation.** The exact same scene redrawn as a richly detailed 1950s–1960s European vintage comic and storybook illustration, combining pen-and-ink engraving and woodcut-inspired linework with a hand-printed, nostalgic feel, keeping framing, positions, proportions, poses, perspective, horizon and scene structure.
- **Line treatment.** Bold, confident black ink outlines with exceptionally fine parallel hatching and cross-hatching for shadows, depth and texture; dense lines in dark areas, lighter spacing in highlights, directional strokes following buildings, roads, clothing, foliage, clouds and architectural surfaces.
- **Colors.** Saturated teal-cyan skies and cool shadows contrasted with warm vermilion red, orange, golden ochre, cream and sun-yellow; olive and emerald greens for vegetation; flat printed colors with subtle risograph grain and vintage ink texture.
- **Accuracy/text rules.** Preserve original cloud shapes, number, positions, weather and lighting mood — never invent clouds or change lighting; keep all original people, buildings, vehicles, objects and recognizable features; simplify faces into vintage comic form while preserving identity, clothing and poses; add no unrelated objects; omit illegible signage rather than generating random text; the upper half must stay photographic and the lower half must be fully redrawn, never a filtered photograph.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption invites readers to open the images and look carefully at the lines — especially the lower half's sky and asphalt road, drawn stroke by stroke, and the arched canopies at the Cijin market. The top half keeps the original street scene; the lower half redraws the same composition — buildings, streets and people — as a nostalgic 1960s retro picture, turning the photo's light and shadow into fine, dense lines, paired with teal-blue, warm yellow and orange-red, giving the flavour of a 1950s–60s European old picture book. The author's comment adds the usual invitation: the full GPT prompt is there to copy and try with your own photos, results are welcome in the comments, and readers are asked to follow and share.
