## The prompt

The source post's first comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it for
followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above;
what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the original
photograph faithfully with only subtle refined grading. The bottom half
extracts only the most recognizable subjects, silhouettes, poses,
objects and narrative connections and reinterprets them as a
limited-color woodcut / linocut impression floating on warm paper —
rebuilt through hand-carved language: visible carving marks, uneven
line thickness, hesitant cuts, rough edges, abrupt turns, small breaks
and gaps, irregular negative space, slight misregistration, worn areas,
dry texture, incomplete printing, missing ink, coarse grain. Complex
backgrounds and clutter are removed; the composition keeps one strong
visual anchor, generous negative space, and incomplete edges that
dissolve into broken ink and exposed paper rather than forming a
rectangular frame. Color is drawn from the photo's most recognizable,
emotionally meaningful colors, reinterpreted in a fresh, soft, bright
limited palette — cream white, ivory, pale pink, mist green, light
blue, soft yellow, pale orange, mint, gentle coral, light ochre, muted
teal — low-to-medium saturation, high brightness, matte physical inks
in separate slightly translucent layers; dark, muddy or heavy vintage
schemes are avoided. A short auto-generated title plus small
keywords/micro-notes are part of the composition. Excluded: generic
filters, full-background replication, polished vector, clean digital
outlines, glossy rendering, cartoon styling, 3D, heavy grunge, cheap
retro, formulaic travel-poster styling.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's first comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents a versatile set of prompts
that turn photos into woodcut prints — people and movement, grand
landscapes, and city scenes all suit it. It keeps the most memorable
thing in a photo — a street-side snack stall, a scooter-lined alley,
the little truck that repairs window screens and replaces glass, a
vinyl record being played — and re-makes it in rough hand-carved
lines: imperfect edges, deliberate breaks and missing ink, as if really
carved knife stroke by knife stroke. The colors are not heavy or
retro: a few evocative colors from the photo are remade in softer blue,
green, pink, yellow and cream. He tested street scenes, night markets,
old shops, parent-child moments, forest, seaside, convenience stores
and everyday objects and could not pick a favorite. Readers are invited
to copy the prompt from the images / first comment, share their
results, and follow for the next prompt.
