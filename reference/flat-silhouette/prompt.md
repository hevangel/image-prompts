## The prompt

The source post's first comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it for
followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above;
what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the original
photograph faithfully with only subtle editorial grading. The bottom
half — "Flat Silhouette Still Life on a Dark Ground" — keeps only the
essential subject and a few closely related elements, rendered as
solid, outline-free, detail-free silhouettes that overlap into one
compact layered cluster on a continuous dark background, with generous
dark negative space (especially above) and the opposite side left open.
Depth comes only from overlap and subtle tonal differences: no
perspective, gradients, realistic lighting or cast shadows. The
palette is drawn from the photo's own emotional colors — its darkest
color deepened (with a trace of hue kept) becomes the ground; the rest
are desaturated into dusty pastel-gray blocks; and exactly one
silhouette is elevated to a bright warm off-white as the single focal
point. Everything carries a delicate dry-gouache powdery grain, matte
and velvety, with soft slightly irregular hand-cut edges. A single
small editorial word in thin uppercase serif, drawn from something
specific in the photo, sits in the dark space beside the cluster.
Excluded: complete scene replication, visible outlines, equally bright
shapes with no focal point, candy colors, pure black, 3D rendering,
cartoon aesthetics, and any distress or aging effects.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's first comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents a set of prompts that turn
photos into flat silhouettes: pleasing and durable to look at, best for
landscapes or close-ups with a clear subject and crisp outlines, and
unsuited to photos with strong light and shadow because the ground is
a deep dark base. The top half keeps the photo; the bottom half
reorganizes the subject into flat silhouettes on the dark ground, and
scattered elements converge into one stacked cluster once the
background is removed — its strength is re-editing, so even messy
photos with small subjects work if you seize the single most important
subject. Readers are invited to copy the prompt from the images /
first comment, share their results, and follow for the next prompt.
