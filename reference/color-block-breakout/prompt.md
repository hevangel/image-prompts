## The prompt

The full GPT prompt (in English) sits in the author's comment on both the set post and the reel. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns one uploaded photo into a standalone "premium editorial comparison poster":

- **Layout.** Strict 3:4 vertical; the canvas splits into two exactly equal horizontal halves (50/50), joined directly at the midpoint. No borders, divider lines, arrows, Before/After labels, logos or watermarks.
- **Top half.** The original photo preserved as a photograph: subjects, faces, poses, proportions, clothing, interactions, textures, lighting and color mood kept; only a subtle editorial grade. No added text.
- **Bottom half — selection.** Analyze only the current photo (never combine photos or reuse subjects, colors or design elements from earlier images). Pick its strongest subject, silhouette, gesture and relationship; compress 2–5 memorable cues into one dominant motif plus at most one supporting element. Remove roughly 85–90% of secondary background, objects, texture and clutter.
- **Bottom half — style.** Bold simplified silhouettes, a few flat shapes, selective contours, and paper-white negative space — recognizable at phone size. About 65–75% of the lower panel stays clean warm-white paper, with generous margins and a visible paper gap at the midpoint.
- **Palette.** At most two ink colors, both drawn only from the current photo's subject or defining cues, plus warm-white paper. The lighter, quieter ink is used for the painted field; the darker, stronger ink for the focal subject. Colors stay mostly flat and matte — no black outlines, no accent colors, no complex gradients, no realistic shading, no noticeable third color.
- **The breakout.** One compact, roughly rectangular painted field (about 20–25% of the lower panel, with soft irregular handmade edges — not a frame or cutout) holds most of the motif. Exactly one meaningful, source-derived part — flowing hair, an extended hand, a wing, a leg, a branch, an instrument, an architectural contour — extends clearly beyond one selected edge as one continuous, readable silhouette. That single extension is the poster's main surprise.
- **Avoid list.** Side-by-side layouts, extra panels, tracing the whole photo, a monochrome filter, invented gestures, distorted anatomy, multiple competing extensions, floating decorative fragments, and any lower-panel element touching or crossing into the upper photo.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) introduces a set of prompts for letting the main subject extend out of color blocks: simplify the photo so the subject breaks out of the block and becomes more memorable. The top half keeps the original photo; the bottom half keeps only the most memorable part — a person's action, a building's outline, a distinctive landscape shape — redrawn from one or two main colors in the photo with simple blocks and lines. The distinctive feature is a small, paint-brush-like color block in the lower half holding most of the illustration, with a small part of the subject naturally extending out of it — a hand, floating hair, a bridge's outward reflection — giving a slight break-the-frame feeling, extra depth and fun, while the simplicity makes the subject's outline and action stand out. It suits photos with clear outlines, actions or distinctive colors. The full prompt is in the images / first comment, ready to copy; readers are invited to follow for the next prompt. Example photos are credited to Pexels.

## How this relates to neighboring entries

This is another pass at the same comparison-poster family as `comparison-poster` (Aug 31, 2026), `clean-illustration` (Oct 8, 2026) and `fresh-illustration` (Oct 23, 2026), filed separately because its prompt differs on points that change the output: instead of a full-width illustration panel, the lower half is mostly empty warm-white paper with a single small painted color block, in at most two inks drawn from the photo, and exactly one part of the subject breaks out of that block. It also forbids black outlines, where `fresh-illustration` requires clean black outlines.
