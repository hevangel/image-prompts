## The prompt

The full prompt sits in photo ⑥'s caption (headed by the style title 扁平幾何人物), in English. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives there, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns each uploaded photograph independently into its own "Flat Geometric Figures" comparison poster on one strict vertical 3:4 canvas, split horizontally into two perfectly equal 50% sections, seamlessly joined, with no borders, dividers or extra panels:

- **Top half — original photography.** A faithful copy of the original photo — subject identity, recognizable features, structure, proportions, pose, object arrangement, authentic textures, natural lighting, shadows, original colour atmosphere and visual mood — with only extremely subtle, refined editorial colour grading. Background areas may be extended naturally if needed; never stretch, distort, repaint, replace or alter the main subject. No text in the top half.
- **Before designing the bottom half — three visual memory points.** Analyse the source photograph and identify (1) the main subject or figure, (2) the most recognizable pose, gesture, interaction or structural arrangement, and (3) the most distinctive object, silhouette, accessory or scene element. These three points found the transformation.
- **Bottom half — flat geometric character poster.** Reinterpret the image as a finished premium flat geometric character editorial poster — not a filter, not a literal copy, not a detailed redraw. Extract only the most recognizable subject, silhouette, structural rhythm, pose logic and narrative feeling, removing 60–90% of secondary details and rebuilding the composition with clean, sophisticated geometric forms. Create one adult character inspired by the original image, built from a few oversized asymmetric geometric shapes, a small head, elongated capsule-like limbs, simple mitten-style hands, and one oversized object naturally integrated into the pose and overall silhouette. Preserve recognizable identity, important visual traits, relevant clothing cues, pose logic and emotional character while simplifying the figure into bold graphic forms. If the source has no person, reinterpret its main subject as a character-like editorial figure while keeping its defining structure, silhouette logic and key visual memory points.
- **Style.** Refined contemporary flat geometric editorial illustration with asymmetric proportions, crisp colour blocking, generous negative space, soft airbrushed shading, subtle dark-to-colour spray gradients only where shapes overlap, fine print grain, and delicate stippling concentrated primarily in darker areas. Calm, balanced, spacious and sophisticated, like a finished designer poster rather than a filter, cartoon sticker, children's drawing or glossy 3D render.
- **Face.** Only minimal black cut-paper facial features, including simple crescent eyes and a minimal mouth. No detailed facial rendering; emotion comes mainly through silhouette, posture, gesture and full-body shape language.
- **Palette.** Strictly six colours: warm off-white #F3F1EB, near-black #10100F, hot pink #F553D2, warm red #F42726, cobalt #293EA1, emerald #13785D. Colours stay flat, graphic and controlled, with soft gradients only where forms overlap; no noisy or overly multicoloured arrangements.
- **Composition.** The lower half must work as a clean standalone editorial poster while remaining visibly connected to the original photo above. The character is the dominant focal point, the oversized object clearly visible and naturally influencing the silhouette, the overall contour strong and immediately readable, the layout balanced with generous negative space.
- **Forbidden.** No extra text, captions, labels, logos, watermarks, Before/After wording, arrows, borders, decorative frames, photorealism in the bottom half, glossy 3D, realistic skin rendering, detailed backgrounds, clutter, extra limbs, distorted anatomy or random objects.
