## The prompt

The full GPT prompt (in English) sits in the author's comment on both the set post and the reel. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns one uploaded photo into a standalone retro-manga editorial advertisement poster:

- **Layout.** Strict portrait format split into two exactly equal horizontal halves (50/50), joined directly at the midpoint. No side-by-side layout, borders, divider lines, labels, logos or watermarks, and all lettering is confined to the lower half.
- **Top half.** The original photo preserved as a photograph: subjects, faces, poses and color mood kept, with only light editorial grading and careful cropping or natural background extension.
- **Bottom half — selection.** Pick the photo's most recognizable subject or gesture; remove most unnecessary background and clutter.
- **Bottom half — style.** Redraw it as a large retro comic advertisement illustration: thick irregular black ink outlines, solid black shadows, white highlights, selective hatching, and simplified flat color.
- **Palette.** A mainly white background with black outlines and shadows, plus one main accent color taken from the photo (a second photo-derived accent only if needed), combined with black-and-white contrast — a retro feel without becoming too old or complicated.
- **The headline.** A short photo-specific English headline, generated for that photo, in enormous bold block lettering filled with the accent color, occupying a large share of the lower panel and layered so the illustrated subject overlaps some letters while staying readable. Subjects sometimes cover letters and letters sometimes emerge behind subjects, with offset shadows for depth and movement.
- **Texture.** Restrained halftone dots, ink grain, hatching, and slight print misregistration.
- **Checklist.** Layout accuracy, photo fidelity, the huge headline, subject–headline overlap, a recognizable illustration, and clean white space.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) introduces a set of prompts for turning photos into Japanese retro manga: unlike the earlier simple, material-focused styles, this one aims for a stronger, more powerful image, with the lower half as a retro-advertising-style comic illustration. It highlights very thick, forceful black lines and very large English lettering that is part of the picture rather than a separate title, with subjects sometimes covering letters and letters sometimes emerging behind subjects, plus thick black outlines and offset shadows for depth and movement. Colors are not a fixed palette: one or two representative colors are taken from the original photo and combined with black-and-white contrast, keeping a retro feel without becoming too old or complicated. The same treatment on a different photo can produce a very different comic image, like a personalized retro magazine advertisement. Example photos are credited to Pexels.
