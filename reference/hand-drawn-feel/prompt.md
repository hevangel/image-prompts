## The prompt

The source post's author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(written in Chinese, with a Chinese introduction). The creator
publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in that
comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

Each uploaded photo becomes its own independent high-end design
poster, no multi-photo collage, 3:4 vertical, two zones strictly 1:1.
The TOP zone preserves the original photo — subject identity,
structure, pose, real texture, natural light and color atmosphere —
with only slight high-end grading for an art-magazine/exhibition feel;
the environment may extend naturally, but the subject is never
stretched, distorted or altered. The BOTTOM zone extracts only the
most recognizable subject, contours, structure, pose and narrative and
rebuilds them as a naïve hand-drawn mixed-media picture-book
illustration: loose, broken, slightly shaky, irregular hand-drawn lines
with natural, charming imperfections; color built from flat fills,
gouache-like broad strokes, translucent layering, crayon/pastel grain
and a little collage-like material — natural, relaxed, childlike,
gentle, handmade picture-book quality; no rigorous realism, no precise
perspective, not every object painted in full. The composition follows
"large negative space above + small-scale narrative subject in the
middle + one comprehensive color-field base below" （空—主體—基座）:
the subject shrinks to a carefully placed focal point amid deliberate
white space; the single broad base block becomes sea, ground, grass,
mountain, tabletop, shadow field or environmental support per the
photo's meaning, laid in with wide horizontal strokes, light/dark
layering, local show-through white and irregular brush marks. The
model is expressly granted re-direction rights — delete, recombine,
crop, rescale, use white space; the subject may sit off-center, at an
edge, suspended or partly cropped, but the whole stays quiet, relaxed
and balanced. Color: 2–4 of the photo's most recognizable, emotionally
valuable colors unified into a softer picture-book palette (saturation
may drop, brightness rise; small vivid accents allowed; no dirty gray,
no cheap high saturation). Text is fused into the image, not added as
layout: a very few distilled words or short phrases, any language, like
handwritten picture-book notes in the white space or base block.
Excluded: object-by-object redraw, filled frames, complex backgrounds,
realistic modeling, heavy oil-paint feel, cartoon-template feel, 3D
feel, over-decoration.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) says this is not simply turning the
whole photo into an illustration. The top half keeps the original photo
with only slight grading, preserving the people, light, texture and
atmosphere of the moment. Below, AI first understands what in the photo
is truly worth passing on — a surfer's gaze, sand grains in a hand,
leaves beneath a swing, footprints on a beach, or some tiny,
unremarkable detail that makes you remember that day — and deletes
everything unimportant. What remains: loose hand-drawn color blocks,
crayon grain and imperfect strokes, with one large field of color at
the bottom catching the image (it may become sea, grassland, a
tabletop, a mountain, or just an unnamed color); not everything needs
to be drawn. A photo records what actually happened, but with the
details gone the result feels more like a memory — what the mind still
holds after time has passed.
