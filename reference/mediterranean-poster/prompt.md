## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it for
followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above;
what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one before-and-after poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves that connect directly at the midpoint — no
divider, blank space or decoration between them. The top half preserves
the original photograph as faithfully as possible with only extremely
subtle refined grading. The bottom half reinterprets the same photo as
a mid-century Mediterranean travel poster / vintage travel
advertisement illustration: keep the most recognizable elements but
significantly reduce photographic detail, simplifying into clear,
clean, rhythmic large flat color areas. The palette is highly saturated
— cobalt and Mediterranean blue, cream white, ivory, forest and olive
green, yellow ochre, terracotta orange-red — reconfigured to the photo's
own color relationships rather than forcing colors that don't belong.
Large environmental surfaces become bold negative-space color fields;
clouds become large soft graphic cream-white forms with a few delicate
curved lines; distant scenery gets atmospheric perspective through
layered flat-color bands. People and objects are simplified silhouettes
with limited shading and minimal linework, while the photo's important
structural lines — roads, railings, horizons, sightlines, coastlines,
mountain contours — are preserved as strong leading lines. Texture is a
refined vintage lithograph / screen-print grain: extremely subtle paper
grain, slight ink irregularities, gentle registration imperfections —
kept bright, clean and fresh, never dirty, damaged, heavily distressed
or overly faded. The mood is the romantic, sunny, relaxed nostalgia of
1930s–1960s European travel posters, while remaining immediately
recognizable as the same place and story. No typography in the output.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents a prompt that turns
ordinary photos into Mediterranean travel posters from old movies. The
top half keeps the original photo; the bottom half keeps the most
memorable parts of the same scene — the rear-view-mirror figure, the
seaside highway, the old van, the sky and horizon — reorganized into
simpler, more rhythmic large color blocks. The colors are part of the
charm: Mediterranean blue, ivory white, olive green, terracotta
orange-red; the whole looks like a 1930s–1960s European travel poster,
all sunshine, sea breeze and leisurely romantic nostalgia, with the
fine print grain and slightly uneven ink kept clean rather than dirty.

comment, post their results in the comments, share, and follow for
more AI image ideas.
