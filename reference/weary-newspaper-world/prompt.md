## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the post body (a long detailed version, under "📌完整GPT指令") and, in a shorter condensed form, in the author's first comment on the set post (under "📌GPT指令"). The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives there, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns each uploaded photograph independently into its own "Weary Newspaper Small World" comparison poster on one strict vertical 3:4 canvas, split horizontally into two perfectly equal 50% sections, seamlessly joined, with no borders, dividers or extra panels:

- **Top half — original photography.** A faithful copy of the original photo — all important subjects, faces, hairstyles, clothing, poses, animals, objects, composition, perspective, natural lighting and authentic colours — with only subtle colour grading and minimal cropping or background extension allowed. Do not redraw, distort, mirror or remove important subjects.
- **Bottom half — newspaper-collage illustration.** The same subjects reinterpreted as a collage on clean warm off-white paper with subtle grain and generous negative space (60–70% negative space; the collage itself kept within about 30–40% of the bottom section). Torn and scissor-cut pieces of old yellowed newspaper, glued on, with visible torn fibrous edges, rough scissor cuts, slightly lifted corners, faint glue wrinkles and soft paper shadows. Mainly yellowed greyscale newsprint — columns of tiny unreadable body text, coarse black-and-white halftone photo fragments, faded grey tones and thin column rules; the direction of text columns and halftone is used to suggest hair, fabric, fur and volume. A few sparse hand-drawn charcoal pencil lines only where needed to clarify outlines.
- **Colours.** Automatically extract 1–2 muted signature colours from the photo, introduced only as a few pieces of faded coloured advertisement or tissue paper, for the most recognisable clothing or feature.
- **Characters.** People and animals become recognisable, oversized-head, short-bodied, slightly asymmetrical 2–3-head-tall chibi characters, assembled from overlapping newspaper scraps.
- **Facial features (marked "MOST IMPORTANT").** The face is a pale, lightly printed newsprint area. Half-closed eyes from small black halftone dots or punched-out dark scraps beneath narrow torn-strip eyelids set at slightly different angles; short crooked eyebrows from thin cut text-line strips; a tiny offset paper-chip nose; a small, flat, slightly tilted mouth cut from a column rule. Moderate facial misalignment — about 70% recognisable structure, 30% playful collage displacement — producing tired, deadpan, mildly unimpressed expressions, each character's expression subtly different. Preserve each subject's defining features, species, clothing, pose, accessories and relationships.
- **Objects.** Where appropriate, recreate one authentic object from the photograph as an oversized newspaper cut-out at 1.5–2.5× the character's size for gentle visual absurdity, without inventing new objects; add no more than two tiny loose newspaper scraps as quiet accents when suitable.
- **Slogan.** One original 8–16-character Traditional Chinese micro-poem inspired by the photograph, gentle, innocent and slightly world-weary in humour, written small in soft pencil handwriting in 2–3 short lines in the negative space. Prioritise correct Taiwanese Traditional Chinese characters; omit the text if accuracy cannot be guaranteed. All newspaper text must remain tiny, blurred and unreadable; never form legible headlines, real names, dates or brand names. Do not reproduce original logos or signage.
- **Forbidden.** No clean digital collage, sharp vector shapes, glossy rendering, anime eyes, neon colours, creepy realistic cut-out photo faces, crowded backgrounds, extra subjects, invented scenery, English text, watermarks, additional panels, borders, logos or readable newspaper text.
- **Mood.** A quiet page from a vintage scrapbook made on a slow Sunday morning — papery, nostalgic, slightly melancholic and deadpan funny.

## English translation of the introduction
