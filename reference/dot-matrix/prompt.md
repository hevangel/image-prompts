## The prompt

The source post's author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(written in Chinese, with a Chinese introduction). The creator
publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in that
comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The shared two-zone template applies (each photo its own independent
high-end design poster, 3:4, zones strictly 1:1; top = original photo
preserved with slight high-end grading only). The BOTTOM zone is a
post-digital experimental poster built by bitmap rasterization +
character/halftone reconstruction + experimental typography. It does
not copy the photo wholesale or redraw object by object; it actively
deletes most background and detail, keeping only the structure,
movement and visual memory points that best represent the original,
rebuilt through bitmap rasterization, dithering, typographic halftone
and character-based imaging so the correspondence with the photo above
reads at a glance. The bottom image itself must be BUILT from
characters, symbols, dot matrices, halftone dots and low-resolution
raster units — not continuous color blocks: dark areas are denser
characters/dots, light areas sparser arrangements plus white space,
with clear hierarchy, rhythm and order (from afar a strong simple
overall image; up close it is made of characters and dots). Full
re-direction rights: the subject need not be complete or centered; it
may be off-center, edge-hugging or partly cropped — "small-scale
subject + huge negative space"; white space is a deliberate
compositional language. Typography is a CORE component: one
distinctive bitmap experimental title plus very few auxiliary texts,
numbers, short phrases or notes; text and image interlock, interleave,
misalign or overlap — true experimental layout, not a title pasted on
afterwards. Color: a restrained black/white/gray base plus 1–3 of the
photo's most recognizable colors extracted as accents (applied to the
title, a halftone layer, a character-density layer or local image
areas); lo-fi digital texture, scan feel, noise and compression feel
allowed. Overall: Post-digital Experimental Typography Poster / Bitmap
Typography / Typographic Halftone / Dithered Image. Excluded: realistic
redraw, keeping the full original scene, filled backgrounds,
information pile-up, ordinary title posters, smooth vector template
feel, color-noise flooding, excessive glitch, mess, cheap effects.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) says this time it's a retro digital
dot-matrix image — not simply turning the photo into an illustration,
nor applying a poster template. The top keeps the original photo with
slight grading only. The bottom is redesigned: AI first analyzes what
the photo's true subject is — a person's pose, a noisy market, a solo
trip, or some small instantly recognizable visual feature — deletes
everything unimportant, then rebuilds what remains from characters,
dot matrices, halftone dots and type, using character density for light
and dark and a low-resolution raster to rebuild contours, with white
space treated as crucial — the subject made small, offset, cropped,
even pushed to the frame's edge amid large space. From afar it first
reads as a clean, well-composed photo; up close the image is made of
characters, dots and low-resolution pixels. Same photo: the top is how
it looked in the moment, the bottom is the visual memory AI chose to
keep. Suits travel photos, street shots, people, architecture, even
imperfect casual snaps.
