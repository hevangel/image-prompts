## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the post body (a long detailed version) and, in a shorter condensed form, in the author's first comment on the set post. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives there, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns each uploaded photograph independently into its own "Original Photography × Crayon Little World — Deadpan Chibi Edition" comparison poster on a strict vertical 3:4 canvas, split horizontally into two perfectly equal 50% sections, seamlessly joined, with no borders or dividers:

- **Top half — original photography.** A faithful copy of the original photo — all important subjects, facial identities, expressions, clothing, poses, objects, composition, perspective, natural lighting and authentic colours — with only subtle colour correction, proportional cropping or natural background extension allowed. Do not redraw, distort, mirror or remove important subjects.
- **Bottom half — crayon little world.** A minimalist hand-drawn crayon world on clean warm ivory paper. Automatically extract the most recognizable subject silhouette from the photo and reinterpret it as one large, softly irregular, pale pastel silhouette made of repeated rough wax-crayon strokes, loose grainy edges and visible paper texture. Place tiny childlike illustrations inside the lower area of this shared silhouette. Silhouette plus illustration together occupy only about 35–45% of the bottom section, leaving 55–65% generous negative space. (The condensed comment version keeps the 55–65% negative space but drops the 35–45% figure.)
- **Characters.** All important people and animals as imperfect 2–3-head-tall deadpan chibi characters — oversized heads, tiny uneven limbs, asymmetrical proportions, half-closed eyes, blank stares, subtly bored expressions, each character slightly different. Preserve recognizable hairstyles, glasses, clothing colours, accessories, relative positions and interactions.
- **Crayon rendering.** Authentic rough wax-crayon marks, uneven broken outlines, imperfect colouring, visible paper grain, loose scribbles, the naive drawing quality of a 4–6-year-old child using their non-dominant hand. Avoid polished digital illustration, clean vector shapes, perfect geometry and detailed shading.
- **Objects and colour.** Keep buildings, food, plants and other inanimate objects non-anthropomorphic. Optionally enlarge one meaningful object actually present in the photograph to 1.5–2.5 times the size of a small character, for gentle visual humour. Simplify all backgrounds and use only a few authentic photo-derived crayon colours. Optionally include one tiny irregular crayon light spot when supported by the original lighting.
- **Slogan.** Add one original 8–16-character Traditional Chinese micro-poem inspired by the photo, arranged in 2–3 small handwritten lines, in the bottom section only. Use accurate Taiwan Traditional Chinese characters; if correct typography cannot be guaranteed, omit the text rather than generate incorrect characters.
- **Forbidden.** No extra subjects, invented objects, copied logos, additional panels, borders, watermarks or Simplified Chinese characters.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) opens by saying that this time the childlike fun of crayon drawing has been added to the world-weary look — last time the people in the photos became deadpan Q-version characters, and this time they have changed into a different drawing style: thick crayon lines, crooked outlines, just like being a kid holding a crayon, drawing whatever you wanted, not caring at all whether the lines were straight. What the creator wanted is exactly that slightly clumsy, wobbly, yet very childlike hand-drawn feel. Inside a pale silhouette live a group of little characters who don't want to try: the bottom half uses one big pale crayon silhouette to hold the tiny deadpan Q-version crayon characters — big heads, short little limbs, eyes still wearing that couldn't-care-less look — then deliberately enlarges a small object from the photo a little, and pairs it with one short deadpan slogan.
