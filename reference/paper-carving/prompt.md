## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(written in Chinese, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and
video). The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text
lives in that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural
summary.

The prompt asks for one independent high-end minimalist design poster
per photo, never combining photos, on a 3:4 canvas in two equal halves.
The top half preserves the original photograph (structure, real
texture, natural light/shadow, original color atmosphere) with slight
high-end grading. The bottom half extracts the most recognizable
contours, structure, spatial relationships and narrative features and
reconstructs them as layered paper cut art / 3D papercraft diorama /
architectural relief — never copying photo details: multi-layer paper
pieces, cut contours, hollowed structures, spatial layering, precise
edges, subtle inter-layer shadows, with a light paper /
architectural-model quality rather than a real 3D rendering. There is
a single visual center, large negative space, and a restrained,
balanced composition. Colors are the photo's brightest, freshest, most
alive hues. Typography is editorial: a large English title, a number,
and 2–3 micro-lines.

> Full prompt text: see the source posts' author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) shares an instruction that turns an
ordinary photo into paper carving. This time the AI's texture isn't
just swapping a treatment — the frame is split into upper and lower
halves: the upper half keeps the original photo; the lower half has
the AI extract what's most worth keeping — a seaside sunset glow, an
alley leading to the sea, a train, a boat, a wind turbine — delete
unimportant detail, keep the most recognizable parts, and turn them
into paper pieces cut out layer by layer. The result looks like a
paper-carving model with a paper-relief feel: even though it's a flat
image, you can see the depth of stacked, hollowed, cut paper, as if
someone remade the world inside the photo with paper. Colors are
picked from the photo's most alive hues — sky blue, sea green, street
white, sunset orange — so the whole thing is clean and comfortable,
never gray. No design knowledge is needed: drop a photo into ChatGPT
and paste the prompt from the images / first comment.
