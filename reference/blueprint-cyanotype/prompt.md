## The prompt

The source post's first comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it for
followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above;
what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the original
photograph faithfully with only subtle editorial grading. The bottom
half reinterprets the photo's actual content as a handmade cyanotype
print on visibly textured flat paper: deep Prussian blue as the
exposed-paper ground, and the main subject — plus only those
environmental elements genuinely belonging to the scene — appearing in
luminous pale ivory, as if exposed by light. The method is editing,
reduction, extraction, simplification and reconstruction: keep the
strongest structures, most recognizable silhouettes, essential
gestures and spatial relationships, with generous negative space. A
strict exclusion defines this prompt: no fixed botanical template —
never add decorative ferns, wildflowers, branches, grasses, leaves,
botanical borders or plant silhouettes to fill space; plants appear
only if genuinely present in the original, and nothing may be invented.
The cyanotype character must be physical: paper fibers, subtly
imperfect contact-print edges, nuanced blue variation, localized
uneven exposure, faint chemical processing marks — not a digital
illustration or a simple blue filter. No frame, lettering, symbols,
logos or watermarks anywhere.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents the prompt that turns a
photo into a "blueprint": the blue is Prussian blue — very deep, rich,
with a faint green tint. In the bottom half the background becomes deep
blue while the subject slowly appears as if light had exposed it onto
paper, and the paper fibers, uneven exposure and small edge
imperfections are deliberately not cleaned up. He likes Prussian
blue's calm elegance. Readers are invited to copy the prompt from the
images / first comment, say which photo they would try, share their
results, and follow for the next prompt.
