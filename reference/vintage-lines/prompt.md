## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and reel).
The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the original
photograph faithfully with only subtle, sophisticated grading. The
bottom half extracts the photo's most recognizable subject, silhouette,
structure, pose and narrative relationships and reconstructs them as a
mid-century vernacular graphic illustration + schematic line drawing +
two-color spot printing. It does not completely trace or replicate the
photograph: diagrammatic abstraction compresses complex objects into a
small number of clear geometric shapes, functional contours and
essential structural elements — like an illustration from an old
instruction manual, a vintage advertising card, a technical label or
vernacular printed material — immediately recognizable, yet more
direct, humorous and symbolic than the realistic object. If a structure
can be explained with a single line, no unnecessary strokes are used:
direct black pen outlines with rectangles, circles, diamonds, straight
lines and simple flat planes, over black linework plus two main colors
from the photo. People, animals and everyday objects may have slightly
exaggerated proportions; machines, buildings and complex structures are
simplified into clear boxes, openings, panels and silhouette
relationships. Perspective is flat, schematic and diagrammatic rather
than realistically lit or spatially deep. The composition is reorganized
around the photo's most important narrative relationship — one clear
primary subject plus only a few supporting objects, guide lines,
directional relationships or structural symbols — with close attention
to visual hierarchy, geometric order, scale contrast, positive and
negative space and intentional whitespace. The result may feel slightly
naïve, awkward, imperfect or handmade in a vernacular way, but never
cluttered: every visual element must exist to explain what is happening
rather than serve as decoration.

## English translation of the introduction

The set caption (Traditional Chinese) says this one is a bit different
from the earlier posts: the photos get a vintage-advertising flavor —
the top half keeps the original photo, the bottom half re-reads the same
photo into the kind of picture seen in old advertisements. The method
is interesting: if one line can say it clearly, don't process it with
many lines; complex houses, machines and cars can be slowly taken apart
into boxes, circles, straight lines and simple color blocks, leaving
only black linework plus two main colors. He loves the final feel — a
bit retro, a bit handmade, even deliberately a little clumsy — like the
small illustrations in old product manuals or an advertising card kept
for many years. The reel caption covers the same ground more briefly
(breakfast shops, street scenes and ice-cream carts all come apart into
simple squares, circles, straight lines and color blocks). Readers are
invited to copy the prompt from the images / first comment, comment
with the photo they would try, share with friends, and follow for the
next prompt.
