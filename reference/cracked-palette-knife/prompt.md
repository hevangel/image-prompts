## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the post caption and identically in the author's first comment on the set post. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives there, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns each uploaded photograph, separately and never merged, into a premium "Original Photography × Palette Knife" artwork on a strict 3:4 vertical canvas split horizontally into two exactly equal halves:

- **Layout.** Top half: the original photo, unchanged in natural color and composition, as the factual reference. Bottom half: a complete hand-painted palette-knife oil painting — a real repainting of every object, line and shadow, never a filter or color shift.
- **Bottom half — transformation.** Visible palette-knife marks, a fine craquelure crack pattern covering the whole painting surface like aged oil paint, and a thick impasto texture. Keep the original layout, proportions, depth and vanishing points; preserve the key subject's pose, shape and color identity; simplify or remove clutter; keep light and shadow soft and consistent. Each poster needs its own clear focal point and strong, natural color contrast. The cracks are dense and uniform across the painting, subtly enhancing it without becoming noise or obscuring the subject. Colors stay bright and warm, edges crisp, paint visibly thick. No 3D rendering, no stock-image style.
- **Text and frame.** Finish with a large, striking English title in elegant classic serif capitals and a small italic subtitle in an art-museum tone; a thin, elegant light-gray double-line frame around the painting only; and a small clean neutral sans-serif "AI GENERATED IMAGE" line centered at the very bottom. (Note: the nine archived examples show no visible title text — the prompt's title instruction does not appear in the posted examples.)
