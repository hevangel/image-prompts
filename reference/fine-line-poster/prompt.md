## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and reel).
The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium minimalist poster per
photo, on a 3:4 canvas in two equal halves that connect naturally at
the midpoint — no blank gap, divider lines, shadows or decoration. The
top half preserves the original photograph faithfully with only
extremely subtle elegant grading. The bottom half rebuilds the photo
from its 2–5 strongest visual memory points, removing roughly 70–90%
of secondary information, in deliberately fragile fine lines instead of
complete outlines: lines independent rather than continuous, with
natural breaks, pauses, fading and reconstructed passages, never fully
closed; ink density uneven, warmer and darker at emotionally important
places, with a subtle natural bleeding as water media soaks into paper.
Color is extremely restrained — the photo's most natural, quiet
inherent colors, significantly desaturated and softened toward low
contrast (pale blue-gray, soft beige-brown, muted pale green, faint
ochre, weak ink black, warm white): the memory of the color, not its
saturation. The main subject occupies only about 10–25% of the lower
half, surrounded by roughly 60–80% near-blank warm-white or very pale
ivory ground, with a few thin broken spatial cues (horizon, ground
line, road edge, shoreline, railing, door frame) so the subject does
not float. Faces keep only minimal cues of direction and expression.
Typography: exactly ONE handwritten-style short phrase distilled from
the photo's emotional theme — not a literal description, not
advertising copy — placed in the negative space. Depth comes from
line density, spacing, whitespace and color concentration: no shading,
3D light, paper grain or decorative texture. The final character is
quiet and poetic, like a memory quietly set down. Excluded:
over-completing the drawing, heavy outlines, perfect vector geometry,
cartoon aesthetics, even line weights, realistic shading, background
clutter, mechanical vector tracing.

> Full prompt text: see the source posts' author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese; set and reel carry the same essay)
asks: if a photo were left with only a few lines, would you still
recognize it? This is a simple-line photo treatment, different from
just turning a photo into a generic "minimalist illustration": the top
half keeps the original photo, and the bottom half keeps only the most
memorable part — sometimes an atmosphere, sometimes a relationship, a
distance, a setting sun, a wait in a laundromat, or a brief
companionship — redrawn with very fine, simple lines that re-comb that
feeling. With fewer things, you see more clearly the feeling that truly
remained. Readers are invited to copy the prompt from the images /
first comment, say which photo they would try, share with friends, and
follow for the next prompt.
