## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and reel).
The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent high-end design poster per photo,
on a 3:4 canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the
original photograph with only subtle refined grading. The bottom half
extracts only the most recognizable subject, contours, pose and
narrative relationships — never object-by-object copying — and rebuilds
them as a mixed-media collage on paper: torn and pasted paper pieces,
printed-text fragments, pencil / graphite / ink hand-drawn lines, thin
watercolor, a few color blocks, and much white space. The model may
redirect the composition (subtraction, rearrangement, cropping, scale
changes) to rescue poor photos. The subject stays small against large
white space. Color: the photo's 2–4 most identifiable colors,
moderately brightened. Typography is minimal and editorial, in any
language. Excluded: smooth vectors, 3D, commercial templates, cheap
scrapbook styling.

> Full prompt text: see the source posts' author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) shares a method for
collage-reconstructing photos: the instruction doesn't redraw the photo
— it re-sorts the same photo. The top half keeps the original photo;
the bottom half pulls out the most memorable things in the photo and
rebuilds them into an image combining torn-paper collage and hand-drawn
lines. In the end only the subject's outlines, poses, sense of
relationship, and the photo's most memorable visual memory points
remain — reassembled with torn-paper texture, hand-drawn lines, a few
color blocks and generous white space. Readers are invited to copy the
prompt from the images / first comment and share their own reworked
images.
