## The prompt

The source post's first comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it for
followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above;
what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per uploaded photo —
never a collage — on a 3:4 canvas split into two equal halves. The top
half preserves the original photograph faithfully, with only extremely
subtle editorial grading, and surroundings may be extended but the
subject never altered.

The bottom half is a two-color Risograph editorial illustration. It
starts by finding the photo's three strongest visual memory points,
then removes roughly 60–75% of background and detail and recomposes
what remains as a minimalist illustration — bold geometric shapes, flat
ink areas, open contours, expressive negative space — corresponding to
the photo without tracing it. Only two inks are allowed: deep
ultramarine blue and warm coral red on ivory paper with a subtle fiber
texture; where they overlap, a darker tone appears on its own. The
print character is essential: uneven ink coverage, visible grain,
halftone texture, slight registration offsets between the two colors,
rough edges and handmade imperfections. Excluded: perfect vector lines,
photographic gradients, 3D or photorealistic rendering, polished digital
illustration, and any text, logos or decoration in the illustration.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's first comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents instructions that draw a
photo using only blue and red, with a print-misregistration feel: the
top half keeps the original photo, and the bottom half drops the
complex background and recomposes the scene in deep blue and coral
red. He likes the print texture — the colors are not perfectly flat or
clean, fine grain shows, the lines are a little loose, and where the
offset red and blue overlap, new shades appear naturally. Using very
few colors and shapes gives familiar scenes more character, and each
photo's different focal point gives every illustration its own flavor.
Readers are invited to copy the prompt from the images / first
comment, say which photo they'd try, share their results, and follow
for the next prompt.
