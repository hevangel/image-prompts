## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(written in Chinese, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and
both reel postings). The creator publishes it for followers to copy.
The full text lives in that comment, linked above; what follows is a
structural summary.

The prompt asks for each uploaded photo to become its own independent
high-end design poster, never combining photos, on a 3:4 canvas in two
equal halves. The top half preserves the original photo faithfully
with slight high-end grading; the environment may extend naturally and
the subject is never stretched, distorted or changed. The bottom half
extracts the photo's most recognizable subject, contours, pose and
narrative relationships but does NOT depict the original completely —
it translates them into a small-scale abstract light-and-shadow
graphic: only the most memorable contours, directions, movements or
spatial relationships, re-expressed through soft-focus projection,
blurred silhouettes, translucent color blocks and partial
afterimages/ghosting, so it still corresponds to the photo above but
feels like a memory left by light, not a redrawing. The composition is
an extremely small subject plus extremely large negative space; the
focus may be off-center, edge-touching, suspended or partly cropped,
positioned by the subject's momentum; the rest is deliberately
low-information and quiet — negative space as a true compositional
element, with size contrast, distance relations, blurred edges and
asymmetric balance for airiness, distance and poetry ("weak subject,
strong atmosphere" editorial order); the lower half is never filled.
Light and shadow are hazy, soft and indeterminate — soft diffusion,
bloom, halation, blurred edge transitions; some contours relatively
clear, others dissipating, bleaching or melting into the background,
like a projection through frosted glass, thin mist, curtains or
strong light; uniform blur and crisp realistic outlines are avoided.
Color: the photo's 2–4 most emotionally valuable color relationships
extracted and re-modulated (not mechanically copied); brightness
raised, stimulation lowered, a slight powdery-mist quality kept; one
large soft composite color as an airy base with a cold or warm color
seeping in like light (pairings derived from the photo, e.g.
powder-mist blue + creamy warm white, pale cyan-green + apricot
yellow, soft pink + pale violet, light sand + mist blue); colors are
sun-bleached — translucent, soft, quiet; fixed blue casts, dirty
gray, dark brown, clashing colors, fluorescent colors and cheap
gradients are avoided. Typography is minimal editorial intervention
only, language unrestricted, with no fixed title/numbering preset: a
few words or a short phrase distilled from the photo's subject,
place, action, emotion, memory or metaphor, set small, thin and
widely letter-spaced, annotation-like, placed quietly in the negative
space, at light-shadow edges or on the visual axis, never overpowering
the subject. Overall: an emotional minimalist poster like an art
magazine / cultural brand / exhibition visual, keeping the subject's
core visual soul rather than a complete replica. Excluded: realistic
illustration, filled backgrounds, complex detail, hard-edged
gradients, commercial-ad feel, templated poster feel, cheap filter
look.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) shares a blurry soft-focus photo
treatment — again not redrawing the photo, but letting AI turn it into
a passage of memory left behind by light. The top half keeps the
original photo (people, scene, light, emotion stay real); the bottom
half no longer depicts the original completely — after analysis it
keeps only the single most memorable frame (a city skyline, a
rain-wet street, a span of companionship, a spatial relationship), and
the rest is given to blurred light and shadow, translucent color
blocks and softly diffusing edges, like a scene the light remembered,
settling slowly in the mind. He likes the deliberate empty space — the
frame is not filled, the subject becomes very small, and the
atmosphere grows stronger. People, buildings, travel scenery, pets,
street scenes, plants and everyday objects all work. The full
instruction is in the images / first comment.
