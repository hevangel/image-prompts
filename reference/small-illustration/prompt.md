## The prompt

The reel's author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt (in
English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it for
followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above;
what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium editorial poster per
photo, on a strict 3:4 canvas in two equal halves. The top half
preserves the original photograph — photographic, not illustrated —
with only subtle high-end grading. The bottom half extracts only the
most recognizable subjects, silhouettes and structural relationships
and reinterprets them as a small-scale editorial spot illustration,
aggressively eliminating secondary detail and background. Its defining
principle: an exceptionally small illustration surrounded by an
enormous amount of untouched paper — the drawing occupies only a small
portion of its half, placed by the subject's natural direction and
weight, with no background, panel, border or frame, and the paper
itself as an active compositional element; when in doubt, remove more.
The drawing uses restrained expressive dry media — wax crayon, oil
pastel, coarse graphite, China marker — a few powerful strokes,
simplified shapes, broken contours, scuffs, incomplete coverage and
visible paper gaps: tactile, handmade, slightly imperfect. Color is
strictly three elements: warm off-white paper, charcoal black carrying
the structure, and rust red used only as a sparse selective accent.
Typography is minimal and optional — a few quiet words in charcoal or
rust red in the negative space, omitted if they don't help. Excluded:
complete scene reconstruction, full-background illustration,
photorealistic redrawing, polished vector looks, multicolored
rendering, oversized headlines.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents a "less is more" small
illustration style: he fine-tuned the earlier illustration prompt for
this one. The top half keeps the original photo for comparison; the
bottom half does not redraw the photo but deletes most of its
information, keeping only the most recognizable outlines, poses and a
few key features, shrunk into a tiny small illustration. The beauty is
in what is not drawn — a small figure surrounded by a large warm-white
paper area, no filled-in background or packed color blocks, lines
allowed to break, skew, leave white and show eraser marks, like
crayon, oil pastel or 2B pencil left directly on paper. Colors are
deliberately restricted: charcoal black carries the main structure,
rust red serves only a few visual accents, and the warm gray-white
paper itself is the third color. Readers are invited to copy the
prompt from the comments, share their results, and follow.
