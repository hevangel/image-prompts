## The prompt

The full GPT prompt (in English) sits in the author's comment on both the set post and the video. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns one uploaded photo into a standalone premium editorial comparison poster:

- **Layout.** Strict vertical portrait format split into two exactly equal horizontal halves (50/50) with a clean straight boundary. No side-by-side layout, no third section, and no titles, text, labels, arrows, dividers, borders, logos, or watermarks.
- **Top half.** The original photo preserved as a photograph: subjects, faces, poses, and color mood kept, with only subtle natural editorial grading and minimal cropping (extending the environment if needed, without distortion).
- **Bottom half — selection.** An entirely new, highly abstract visual translation using the same photo as the sole visual source. Do not trace the scene, reconstruct the complete scene, or apply a conventional illustration filter. Identify the most recognizable subject, gesture, relationship, silhouette, structure, direction, or memory point, and select one defining feature — adapted by subject type (portraits: head contours, hairstyle direction, shoulder lines, profiles; actions: gesture direction, movement, balance; relationships: number, relative positions, contact points, interactions; architecture: rooflines, arches, towers, steps, proportions; landscapes: mountain contours, tree silhouettes, coastlines, horizons, water flow; animals: ears, head shapes, tails, wings, postures; everyday objects: outlines, handles, curves, structural features).
- **Bottom half — composition.** The motif is centered or slightly off-center and occupies roughly 25–40% of the lower half's visual area, surrounded by generous clean negative space; it is not enlarged to fill the lower half. The background is warm ivory, soft off-white, or pale beige, with no prominent texture.
- **Palette.** 3–5 muted colors chosen independently for each photo, based on its recognizable subject colors, light–dark contrasts, and emotional atmosphere, reinterpreted as soft modernist gouache tones. Never a fixed palette, never colors reused from another photo.
- **Painted aesthetic.** Minimal hand-painted gouache with subtle paper-collage qualities — flat, matte, soft, slightly translucent color shapes, gently irregular edges, delicate hand-painted marks, subtle pigment variation, and minimal overlapping brushstrokes. Avoid strong grain, realistic paper fibers, heavy oil-paint textures, complex gradients, volumetric shadows, embossing, sculptural 3D paper layers, detailed outlines, and photographic textures.
- **Abstraction limits.** The result must remain highly abstract — not a complete character sketch, cartoon, architectural line drawing, realistic watercolor, complex collage, or decorative pattern.
- **Self-check.** About 95% detail reduction; no more than 8–15 major shapes; 25–40% motif coverage; abundant negative space; independently selected colors; preservation of the essential visual memory point; and complete absence of text or unnecessary decoration.
- **Intended contrast.** Richly detailed reality above versus quiet, minimalist abstraction below.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) shares a set of instructions for turning photos into an abstract gouache feel. The upper half keeps the original photo; the lower half uses a small amount of soft colors, together with a few incomplete shapes and lines, to recombine the features in the photo. This set of instructions leaves a large amount of blank space: it does not fill the picture, and does not need to be drawn very carefully; sometimes it is just a few blocks of color, even incoherent, yet you can still tell what the original photo wanted to convey. The author comment introduces the full GPT prompt for "abstract gouache texture" and invites followers to copy it, try it with their own photo, and share results in the comments. Example photos are credited to Pexels.

## How this relates to neighboring entries

This is a new branch of the comparison-poster family (see `comparison-poster`, `clean-illustration`, `fresh-illustration`, `color-block-breakout`, and `japanese-retro-manga`), filed separately because its lower half is a different treatment: a minimal abstract gouache reduction — a few soft color blocks and incomplete shapes on warm ivory, with about 95% of detail removed — rather than a clean outline illustration, a bold retro-manga poster, or a small color block with a breakout element.
