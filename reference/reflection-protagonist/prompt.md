## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the author's comment on both the set post and the reel. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns one uploaded photo into a vertical comparison poster:

- **Layout.** One standalone vertical image, 3:4, split into two equal horizontal halves — top half a faithful reproduction of the uploaded photo, bottom half the transformed version, with no third panel.
- **Bottom half — transformation.** A dreamy, naive, hand-drawn abstract painting in a Miró-like manner: irregular shapes, soft and defocused edges, gentle glow, and visible screen-print/grain texture, while keeping the scene's recognizable features, characters, and composition.
- **Reflection rule.** Light, highlights, and especially existing wet/reflected surfaces (notably rainy streetlights) are emphasized and enlarged into the main visual subject, not treated as background.
- **Colors.** Muted Morandi tones drawn from the source image, with complementary color accents; no fixed palette is imposed — colors follow the source.
- **Text rules.** No framing, dividing lines, labels, captions, logos, or watermarks, and absolutely no text anywhere in the image.

## English translation of the introduction

The author introduces a complete GPT prompt whose most interesting trick is turning an ordinary rainy street scene into a hazy, dreamlike, slightly playful abstract artwork. The how-to is simple: upload a photo, copy the complete English prompt, and paste it into ChatGPT together with the photo. Rainy street scenes, portrait close-ups, and photos of pets at home are especially recommended. Readers are invited to share results they like in the comments and to follow for more free prompts.
