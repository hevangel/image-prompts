## The prompt

There is **no copyable prompt text** in this post — a first for the catalog. The caption says so directly ("這次沒有⋯⋯（請看置頂留言）" — "there isn't one this time… see the pinned comment"), and the pinned comment points to a shared Gemini Gem instead:

- **Gem:** https://share.gemini.google/tkdT69uaEfU8 — titled "蠟筆繪本風格對比圖生成器" (Crayon Picture-Book Style Comparison Image Generator), published Nov 15, 2026, 5:17 AM. A second share link, https://share.gemini.google/KSzzHsZnJa8c, was also listed on the Gem's share page. The Gem's instruction text is not shown on the share preview (it requires continuing the chat), so the prompt itself is not readable from the post — only its behaviour is described.
- **How it works (3 steps, from the pinned comment):** open the Gemini share link, upload a photo and type "1", and Gemini generates the top-bottom comparison hand-drawn illustration. The author invites readers to come back and share their finished work in the comments.
- **What the Gem is built to do (from the caption):** the author built the settings into structured rules in Gemini's generation flow — crayon × colored-pencil texture, natural hand-drawn strokes and paper grain, a warm and cute picture-book feel, and a top-bottom half-and-half layout with no extra text or labels. The portrait (#4) and snow-scene (#2) photos in the set are the author's own test results.

The structural summary below is therefore drawn from the caption, the pinned comment and the Gem's title — not from a prompt text the author published.

The Gem turns an uploaded photograph into a comparison poster on a vertical 3:4 canvas, split horizontally into two halves:

- **Top half — original photography.** A faithful copy of the original photo, labelled "ORIGINAL" — recognisable subjects, poses, composition and colours, unchanged.
- **Bottom half — crayon × colored-pencil hand drawing.** The same complete scene redrawn as if by a child with crayons and colored pencils, labelled "STYLIZED": strong hand-drawn feel, slightly rough lines, loose and casual colours, visible crayon strokes and paper grain, a warm and cute picture-book feel. The scene's composition and main subjects stay recognisable; no extra text or labels beyond the two half-labels.
- **Layout.** Exactly two halves, original above and hand-drawn below, seamlessly joined — no borders, additional panels, captions, arrows, logos or watermarks.

> Full prompt text: not published — the prompt lives inside the shared Gemini Gem linked above.
