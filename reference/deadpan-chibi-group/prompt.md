## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the post body and in the author's first comment on the set post. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives there, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns each uploaded multi-person photograph into its own independent "Original Photography × Relaxed Hand-Drawn Chibi Illustration" comparison poster on a strict vertical 3:4 canvas, split horizontally into two seamless equal 50% sections — no text, borders, labels, logos or watermarks:

- **Top half — original photography.** A faithful copy of the original photo — subjects, faces, clothing, poses, relationships, composition, lighting and colours — with no changes.
- **Bottom half — relaxed hand-drawn chibi, multi-person.** Auto-detects all primary people and visible pets and reinterprets each as a tiny recognizable 2–3-head-tall hand-drawn chibi character with short limbs, imperfect organic outlines, soft flat colours, deadpan fish eyes, half-closed eyes and subtle awkward humour. It preserves subject count, left-to-right order, relative heights, distinctive details (hair, glasses, clothing), poses and meaningful interactions, adapts spacing and gestures, and never forces separation, unrelated actions or straight-line standing. The small illustrated subjects sit near the lower centre, occupying about 20–30% of the bottom half, with 60–70% clean negative space on a near-white background tinted toward 1–2 colours extracted from the photo.
- **Mood.** Minimal, tender, relaxed and quietly funny. No invented subjects, no extra objects, no decorative backgrounds.
- **Five features listed in the caption.** Auto-detect people/pets; preserve each person's hair, glasses, clothing, height difference and position; dead-fish-eye + relaxed hand-drawn feel; natural multi-person interaction with subtle cold humour when suitable; lots of minimalist negative space.
- **Tip in the caption.** After generating, check person count, faces and positions, and ask the AI to fine-tune if needed.
- **Scope.** An upgrade of the single-person deadpan chibi style for friends, couples, family and pets together in multi-person photos.
