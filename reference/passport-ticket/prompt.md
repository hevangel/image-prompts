## The prompt

The source post's author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(written in Chinese, with a Chinese introduction). The creator
publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in that
comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt specifies that the single uploaded image is the ONLY visual
source. The output is one vertical before/after design in two stacked
panels strictly 50/50, meeting at the midpoint with no gap, border or
divider. The TOP panel is the original image kept pixel-faithful: no
redrawing, retouching, recoloring, or cropping of important content.
The BOTTOM panel condenses the photo's place, culture and memory into
one small refined hand-drawn iconic illustration on warm ivory/cream
old paper (paper fiber, print grain, faded ink), with colors extracted
from the photo and desaturated into vintage pastels, thin vintage
ticket borders, and small serif / typewriter / postmark type, with
lots of white space and a flat front-facing editorial feel. The model
auto-adds the country, city, destination code, mini flag, local flower,
cultural symbols and landmarks, plus micro boarding-pass details
(DESTINATION, CITY, GATE 01, SEAT A12, CLASS EXPLORER, DATE 2026) and a
slightly worn passport stamp that may fuse local architecture,
scenery and totems. The result is explicitly a collectible graphic
souvenir, not a usable document.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents travel memories kept as
paper souvenirs: the top keeps the original travel photo; the bottom
condenses the same journey into a vintage passport / boarding-pass /
souvenir-ticket design after AI reads the photo's city, landmarks,
architecture, culture, clothing and scenery — old paper, faded
printing, low-saturation vintage colors, local flags, flowers,
postmarks and destination codes. The photo records what you saw; the
ticket keeps what you remember.
