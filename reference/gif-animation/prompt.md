## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the author's comment on the set post (and inline in the reel's caption). The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns one uploaded photo into an 8-second GIF animation:

- **Input.** One uploaded photo, optionally plus a target illustration to use as the animation's endpoint — so ChatGPT has both a start and a finish to work from.
- **Duration and motion.** An 8-second GIF; the subject moves slowly and naturally while the scene stays put.
- **Transformation.** Over the course of the animation the image morphs continuously from the real photograph into a vivid abstract illustration — bold colors, rough brushstrokes, screen-print texture — and ends resting on the finished artwork.
- **Fidelity rules.** Keep the original people, scene and composition throughout; the change must be gradual and continuous, never a jump cut.
- **Cleanliness rules.** No text, logos or watermarks.
