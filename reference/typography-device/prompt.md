- **Collected:** Oct 22, 2026
- **Style name:** the caption gives no separate Chinese style name; the style is defined by its core rule, quoted in the caption as **Typography as Image** — text as part of the image's structure.
- **Distinctions:** distinct from `geometric-poster` (shape-driven reinterpretation with a minimal title + year, colors extracted per photo) and from `dot-matrix` (bitmap/halftone character reconstruction on a black/white/gray base) — here the lettering itself forms the structure of a small geometric device, with a FIXED functional color system.

## The prompt

The source post's author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(written in Chinese, with a Chinese introduction). The creator
publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in that
comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The shared two-zone template applies (each photo its own independent
high-end design poster, 3:4, zones strictly 1:1; top = original photo
preserved with slight high-end grading only). The BOTTOM zone extracts
only the most recognizable subject, contours, structure, momentum
（走勢） and narrative relationships — no wholesale copy; irrelevant
detail deleted; the visual memory points recomposed into a
small-volume, center-isolated typographic-graphic device （小體量、
中心孤立式的字體圖形裝置）. The subject may be further geometrized,
modularized and symbolized: any subject translated into lines, frames,
columns, blocks, color fields and a few structural nodes forming a
visual landmark; the correspondence with the photo above must read at
a glance. Composition: "small-scale seal/stamp-like key visual + large
negative space" — the subject occupies only a small area of the bottom
zone, placed freely (centered-offset, suspended, near the central axis,
or partly cropped), surrounded by clean white space so it reads like a
visual device exhibited in a space; white space itself participates in
the composition. Typography follows the "Typography as Image" logic:
text is not a title added afterwards but part of the subject's
structure itself — a very few words distilled from the photo's subject
identity, place, action, emotion or symbolism, any language; text may
be stretched, compressed, split, set vertically, interleaved, embedded
into frames, or serve directly as the structure's skeleton; compact,
sharp geometric lettering with architectural structure. Color: a
high-saturation FUNCTIONAL color-division system on a limited palette —
bright orange = main structure and type skeleton; clear cyan-blue =
information hierarchy and auxiliary blocks; high-brightness yellow =
local highlights and edge emphasis; a little magenta/bright pink =
visual jump-points; restrained black = anchors the visual center.
Slight brightness/saturation adjustment per the photo's color
temperature is allowed, but the color-role relationships must stay
clear. Background: pure warm white or very pale near-white. Final
visual: small centered key visual + architectural typography + modular
geometric construction + high-contrast functional color + large white
space, referencing public cultural visuals, art-festival posters and
architectural visual systems. Excluded: full-bleed composition,
complex backgrounds, multiple equally important focal points, ordinary
text layout, realistic illustration, cartoon feel, 3D rendering, cheap
color clashes, templated poster feel.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) says the top keeps the original real
photo; the bottom does not redraw the photo at all. Instead it only
grasps the subject's contours, structure, momentum and relationships,
and re-decomposes them into type, geometric lines, color blocks,
frames, and a few structural nodes — finally becoming a small,
restrained visual device. He especially likes its large-white-space
feeling: not filling the frame, but a small subject + a large area of
space, so the whole image feels a bit like an art-festival poster, an
architecture exhibition, or an indie publication's key visual. The
colors are fixed into a functional system: orange = main structure,
cyan-blue = auxiliary information, yellow = highlight, magenta / bright
pink = accent jump, black = anchors the visual center. The most
important point: Typography as Image — text is not a title pasted on at
the end, but directly part of the image's structure.
