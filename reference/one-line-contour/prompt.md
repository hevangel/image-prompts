## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and reel).
The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the original
photograph faithfully with only very subtle grading. The bottom half
does not trace or fully redraw the photo: it first identifies the 2–5
strongest visual memory points, removes roughly 70–90% of background,
textures, decoration and secondary detail, and reconstructs what
remains as a minimal continuous contour illustration / one-line
editorial drawing in a mid-century vernacular line style. The drawing
prioritizes the outer contour in extremely economical, flowing, natural
black or dark-charcoal fine lines with a handmade quality: if one line
can explain a structure, no second stroke is added; contours connect
and flow; some areas stay open, broken or intentionally omitted. No
realistic shading, 3D lighting, skin texture, detailed hair or clothing
wrinkles; faces are reduced to the minimum lines for pose and identity;
proportions may feel slightly naïve and gently awkward, but identity,
pose and relationships must stay recognizable — narrative
relationships (contact, eye direction, embraces, shared gestures)
matter more than visual detail. Color is an extremely limited spot
system: the black linework plus exactly ONE muted accent color from
the photo (muted coral, dusty pink, sage green, ochre, soft blue),
applied only to a few selected closed areas or large shapes, on clean
white or very pale warm-white paper with generous negative space — the
subject occupies about half the panel and the linework floats. Any
Picasso-like influence appears only as economy of line, never imitation
or Cubist distortion. No typography in the output. Its closing
requirement, in paraphrase: the lower half must be recognizable as the
upper photo using only a few contour lines and one selective spot
color — a visual memory extracted from the photograph, not a standard
line-art filter.

## English translation of the introduction

The set caption (Traditional Chinese) presents a prompt that keeps the
moment in a photo using just a few lines: find the most evocative part
of the photo and preserve it again with very few lines. What charms him
most is that the AI does not foolishly redraw the whole photograph — it
first finds what carries the most feeling (the moment of mending a
fishing net, a father-and-daughter interaction, a glance back, a bowed
head, or simply a silhouette or a gesture), and the picture moves you
at a glance. What remains is only a few lines, a touch of color and
some white space, yet it is instantly recognizable. Faces need not be
fully drawn, clothing need not account for every wrinkle, backgrounds
need not be copied wholesale; as long as the person's posture survives,
the image works. Color likewise: never fill the whole picture — choose
the single most fitting representative color and lay it lightly on a
few key spots. In his summary: the photograph captures that moment,
but what stays in the heart afterward is the atmosphere of the time,
and emotions you had not even noticed in yourself. The reel caption is
a shorter version of the same message. Readers are invited to copy the
prompt from the images / first comment, say which photo they would
most like to try, share, and follow for the next prompt.
