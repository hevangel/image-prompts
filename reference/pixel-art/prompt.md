## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and reel).
The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium minimalist design poster
per photo, on a 3:4 canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves
the original photograph with only subtle refined grading; the
environment may extend, the subject is never altered. The bottom half
rebuilds the photo as atmospheric pixel art: a low-resolution pixel
language with a limited palette, soft color blocks, clear silhouettes,
pixel dithering and subtle grain — a nostalgic game world / retro RPG
environment rather than a pixel filter. There is high negative space
with a small subject and wide expanses of sky, air, mist or water, in
a pastel retro palette (misty blue, pale cyan, mint green, creamy
yellow, warm beige, soft pink), low-contrast and dreamlike. Typography
is minimal: tiny pixel-style titles and fine annotations. Excluded: a
standard pixel filter, a generic game screenshot, mechanical 8-bit
blocks, photorealistic 3D, cartoon/anime excess, and busy complete
scenes.

> Full prompt text: see the source posts' author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese; the set's is the longer version)
presents a way of turning photos into a "pixel" feeling, but different
from simply making a game screen: the top half keeps the photo; the
bottom half slowly removes complex backgrounds, materials and details
until only posture, silhouette, direction and the one moment most
worth keeping remain — people smaller, more sky, more white space, in
soft blue, green, rice-yellow and pink — like an old RPG scene you
still remember even though nothing happened there. The point is not
pixelating a photo but preserving the remembered image. Readers are
invited to copy the prompt from the images / first comment, try it
with travel photos, street scenes, people, seaside, old houses, pets
or everyday snaps, post their work to share, and follow.
