## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and reel).
The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the original
photograph faithfully with only subtle refined grading. The bottom half
— an "Editorial Character Poster" — does not cartoonize, trace or
redraw the whole photo: it identifies the 2–5 strongest visual memory
points, removes roughly 70–90% of unimportant detail, and rebuilds on a
warm off-white ground in an extremely flat poster language — large
color areas, minimal contours, no texture. Figures keep their
recognizable pose, clothing, movement and colors, but proportions may
be deliberately altered (heads slightly smaller, limbs and height
lengthened) for a relaxed, witty editorial feel; faces are reduced to
minimal marks suggesting direction and expression. The defining rule
is the asymmetric enlargement: in every lower half, exactly ONE
important visual element — a subject, object or major color area — is
enlarged by about +35–60% relative to everything else, clearly breaking
the photo's original proportions without distorting the subject itself;
all other elements keep their relative proportions and must not be
enlarged, or the effect is destroyed. The enlargement serves the
subject's movement, direction, posture and narrative; with no person
present, the most recognizable object, vehicle, building or color area
is enlarged instead. Color: 3–5 dominant colors extracted and
reorganized, flat and even — no gradients, realistic light/shadow,
reflections or complex color. Typography is required: one short English
title from the photo's action, place, state, emotion or metaphor, plus
a few micro-annotations (place, weather, time, state, a short phrase)
forming a poster hierarchy. Overall: quiet, stylish, design-led modern
editorial poster with narrative warmth and a little humor.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese; the set's is the longer version)
presents the prompt that asymmetrically enlarges a photo's subject.
The top half keeps the original photo; the bottom half re-reads the same
photo into a more designed form — not a direct cartoon conversion: the
AI first judges what the photo's protagonist is (a person's action, a
giant surfboard, a shooting pose and the hoop, a row of dim street
lamps), gradually removes unimportant background, and simplifies people
(smaller heads, elongated limbs, clothing as a few large color blocks,
facial features reduced to a hint of expression). The most fun part is
choosing one important element and enlarging it asymmetrically — an
action or pose, an airport timetable, a red skirt hem, a tram with
pedestrians — recombining it with the person into a new silhouette,
with generous white space, a few simple colors and a faint paper/print
feel. Photos with a clear action, or a person interacting with
something, work especially well. Readers are invited to copy the
prompt from the images / first comment, post their results in the
comments, share, and follow.
