## The prompt

The source post's author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(written in Chinese, with a Chinese introduction; the only comment on
the post). The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full
text lives in that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural
summary.

The prompt asks for each uploaded photo to become its own independent
high-end design poster, never splicing photos, on a 3:4 canvas in two
zones strictly 1:1 at 50% each. The top zone preserves the original
photo faithfully with only slight high-end grading; the environment
may extend naturally to fit the frame, but the subject is never
stretched, distorted or changed. The bottom zone extracts only the
most recognizable subject, contour, structure, pose and narrative
relationships — not copying the photo — and rebuilds the emotion,
relationships, direction and visual metaphors most worth keeping as a
minimalist-whitespace conceptual illustration vignette. Most
background and secondary detail is deleted; a very small graphic
subject sits in very large negative space, placed center-low or
off-side, possibly incomplete or partially cropped; white space is
the main compositional element (air, distance, loneliness, time,
pause, psychological space). The visual language is extremely
low-information hand-drawn thin lines with slight handwritten
pauses/irregularity, simple silhouettes, a few trajectories and
symbolic graphics; no realistic volume. Color: a warm gray-beige /
natural aged-paper base plus a single low-saturation cool monochrome
drawing system taken from the photo's temperature (indigo,
blue-violet or gray-violet), layered by value and density only —
quiet, literary, restrained; no rich multicolor, strong clashes or
digital gradients. Text is minimal intervention only, language
unrestricted, with no preset title/numbering: a few distilled words
or a short phrase in small, thin, restrained type with a slight
manuscript/old-print feel, integrated quietly into the white space.
Excluded: complete scene replication, filled frames, complex
backgrounds, realistic depiction, cartoon or 3D feel, vivid
multicolor, commercial-illustration feel, templated poster effect.
The overall aim is poetry-collection / private-manuscript /
psychological-archive quality with lingering resonance.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) shares an instruction that preserves
a photo's lingering afterglow — keep the outline, keep the feeling of
that day. What we want to keep from some photos is not everything in
them: maybe just her back view, a moment waiting for transport,
raindrops on a window after rain, or a bright moonlit night. This time
AI does not redraw the photo; it removes most of it. The top half
keeps the original photo; the bottom half re-analyzes what in the
photo most deserves not to be forgotten and keeps only that — a tiny
outline, a few thin hand-drawn lines, a little low-saturation
blue-purple, everything else left blank. The photo records what
happened that day; the small image below records how that day felt,
remembered after time has passed. Sometimes the more is deleted, the
stronger the remaining feeling. The full instruction is in the images
/ first comment.
