- **Distinctions:** verified distinct from `colored-paper-collage` （色紙拼接 — geometric flat blocks, no paper texture or base band), `paper-collage-lines` (torn paper + hand-drawn lines) and `paper-cut` （高彩度剪紙 — saturated flat cut-outs). ## The prompt

The companion video post's author comment carries the creator's full
GPT prompt (written in Chinese, with a Chinese introduction). The
creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The top zone and the 3:4 / strict 1:1 format follow the shared
two-zone template (original photo preserved above with slight grading).
The bottom zone rebuilds the photo's key subject as a color-field-base
cut-paper collage （色場基座式紙藝拼貼插畫） in a split color-field
composition: large near-white negative space above, ONE solid-color
base band sealing the full width below (no perspective, table edge or
gradient), the subject near the boundary, and an optional local
geometric backdrop block. Shapes are cut paper with hand-cut edges,
paper grain and layering. Colors: 2–4 colors extracted from the photo,
reorganized into soft bright pastel color fields. The model is
explicitly granted re-direction rights. Text is minimal and fused
editorially into the image. Excluded: full scene conversion, complex
backgrounds, realistic perspective, gradients, heavy shadows, filled
frames, cartoon / 3D / template feel.

> Full prompt text: see the source video post's author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) explains that the top keeps the
original photo; the bottom re-judges the most memorable subject,
contours and composition and reorganizes them by subtraction into a
paper-collage illustration with white space, paper-cut contours, paper
layers and soft color blocks — less a photo-to-illustration conversion
than an edited, exhibition-like collectible design poster.
