## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the post caption and identically in the author's first comment on the set post. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives there, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns each uploaded photograph, separately and in upload order, into its own independent premium "Original Photography × Abstract Naïve Risograph Editorial poster" on a strict 3:4 portrait canvas, split horizontally into exactly two equal halves joined at the midpoint — no side-by-side layout, extra panels, divider lines, borders, text, labels, logos, arrows, or watermarks:

- **Top half — original photography.** A faithful copy of the original photo — identity, face, expression, pose, clothing, proportions, objects, perspective, composition, lighting, textures and original color — with only extremely subtle editorial grading. Minimal crop or natural background extension only; no stretching, distorting, repainting, replacing, or altering subjects; no hand-drawn/print effects.
- **Bottom half — naïve risograph illustration.** A casual, retro-grain hand-drawn world: naïve / crayon hand-drawn style, flat abstract colour blocks, wobbly rough outlines, white space, and a retro risograph print look with misregistration, grain/speckles and print flaws, in a bold-but-muted editorial palette. The person's face, expression, pose, outfit, hairstyle and key features — and the main subject, important objects and overall composition — stay recognisably the same.
- **Simplification.** Simplify the background and drop unnecessary background detail, keeping the main subject and important objects clear; the prompt is designed so the AI prioritises keeping the main subject over background fidelity.
- **Finish.** A print-like, editorial-poster finish; one poster per uploaded photo. Generate the finished poster directly, with no questions, explanations, or additional text.

> Full prompt text: see the source post caption / author's first comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

If you like a relaxed, cute, design-savvy style, try this set of instructions! Do you ever feel this way? Some pictures are clearly not drawn precisely, yet they make you want to look again more than "too perfect" pictures. The lines are crooked, the colours aren't neatly filled in, and there's even a feeling of misaligned printing. But strangely, these little imperfections make the whole picture full of character.

The lower half turns the photo into a somewhat naive, casual, retro-grain hand-drawn world. Keep the hairstyle, expression, pose and key features, then reinterpret the whole photo with irregular colour blocks, lines and white space — it looks a bit scribbly, yet scribbly in a very design-savvy way.

This set of instructions has a practical design: it lets AI prioritise keeping the main subject and important objects, and simplifies unnecessary background details. To try it: (1) pick a favourite photo with a clear subject, (2) upload it to ChatGPT and paste the English instruction, (3) you can make a 3:4 vertical poster with the original on top × a hand-drawn illustration below. If the face, pose or colour blocks aren't ideal, add the details you want to the instruction to get closer to what you imagine!

The full GPT instruction is in the post body and the first comment — feel free to copy and play with it! If you like it, share it with friends and follow me, and let's explore more AI creative ideas together!
