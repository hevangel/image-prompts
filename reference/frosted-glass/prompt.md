## The prompt

The source post's pinned comment carries the full GPT prompt (in English, with a
Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it for followers to copy: paste the
prompt into an image model, upload your own photo, and share the result.

Structural summary: one independent poster per uploaded photo, on a strict 3:4
portrait canvas split into two equal horizontal halves. The top half preserves
the original photograph faithfully, with only subtle editorial grading. The
bottom half reinterprets the photo's most recognizable subjects as a minimalist
abstract composition with layered frosted-glass aesthetics — translucent
frosted panes, selective (never uniform) blur and haze, restrained guide lines,
on an ivory background. The palette is drawn from the photo and brightened into
vivid, gentle translucent "healing" colors with warm sunlight accents; muted or
dirty tones, cartoons and plastic textures are explicitly excluded. A short
English title may be integrated in restrained modern typography.

> Verbatim prompt text: see the source post's pinned comment (linked above).
