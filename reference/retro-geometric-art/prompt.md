## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the post body and in the author's first comment on the set post. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives there, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns each uploaded photograph, separately and in upload order (never mixing subjects or elements between photos), into its own independent "Original Photography × Bauhaus Geometric Art" comparison poster on a strict vertical 3:4 canvas, split horizontally into two seamless equal 50% sections — no text, borders, labels, logos or watermarks:

- **Top half — original photography.** A faithful copy of the original photo — composition, framing, lighting, subject, details and colours — with no changes.
- **Bottom half — Bauhaus geometric art.** The prompt automatically identifies the main subject, its distinctive/recognisable silhouette, its pose, and two representative/dominant colours, then reinterprets the same scene with Bauhaus geometry — circles, triangles, rectangles, clean lines and bold flat geometric shapes, in harmonious colours, with restrained black, white and warm ivory accents. Recognisable features, subject identity and spatial relationships are preserved.
- **Texture.** Subtle vintage film grain and a printed-paper / retro print texture, so the poster reads like a century-old art poster; the design stays elegant, balanced and visually striking.
- **Scope.** Works for people, animals, buildings, landscapes and everyday small objects; no design knowledge or manual colour picking is needed — the instruction analyses the photo itself.
