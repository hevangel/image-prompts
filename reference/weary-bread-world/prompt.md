## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the author's comment, in English, under the style title "Original Photography × Baked Dough Little World — Deadpan Chibi Edition". The creator publishes it for followers to copy; the same prompt text also appears in the post caption. The full text lives there, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns each uploaded photograph independently into a premium 3:4 vertical poster split horizontally into two perfectly equal 50% sections, with no borders or extra panels:

- **Top half — original photography.** A faithful copy of the original photo — subjects, faces, clothing, poses, objects, composition, lighting and colours — allowing only subtle grading and minimal cropping or natural background extension. Add nothing, remove nothing, put no text there.
- **Bottom half — baked-dough diorama.** Recreate the subjects as a tiny handmade baked-bread-dough chibi diorama on a warm off-white flour-dusted surface, with soft macro lighting, gentle shadows and generous negative space.
- **Colour.** Warm golden-tan, pale honey and toasted-brown dough, plus only 1–2 muted signature colours taken from the photo for clothing or accessories.
- **Figure style.** Deadpan chibi — 2–3 heads tall, oversized head, short uneven limbs, puffy asymmetrical shapes, with fine crust cracks, tiny blisters, visible dough seams, scored details, twisted dough hair and toasted-brown edges.
- **Faces (flagged "MOST IMPORTANT" in the prompt).** Tired, mildly unimpressed deadpan fish-eye expressions — pale dough-disc eyes, tiny black sesame pupils, uneven heavy eyelids, crooked dough-strip eyebrows, an offset dough-ball nose and a small tilted scored mouth — maintaining "70% recognizable facial structure and 30% playful handmade misalignment".
- **Fidelity.** Preserve every main subject's distinctive features, clothing, pose, accessories and relationships, without inventing subjects.
- **Ground/scale.** The diorama occupies about 30–40% of the bottom section, leaving most of the space empty; when suitable, enlarge one authentic object from the photo to 1.5–2.5 times the character size, sculpted entirely from baked dough; no more than two loose crumbs or sesame seeds.
- **Text.** Add an original 8–16-character Traditional Chinese micro-poem in 1–2 short lines, small, in soft pencil handwriting in the negative space; omit it if accurate Taiwanese Traditional Chinese cannot be guaranteed.
- **Forbidden.** No plastic, CGI or glossy surfaces, no burnt crust, no realistic faces, no anime eyes, no neon colours, no crowded scenery, no invented objects, no English text, logos or watermarks, and no additional panels. Overall feel: quiet, warm, slightly melancholic, handmade, with charmingly awkward deadpan humour.
- **Usage.** Upload a photo to ChatGPT, copy the English prompt, and submit it to generate.
