## The prompt

The source post's first comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it for
followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above;
what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the original
photograph faithfully with only subtle editorial grading. The bottom
half reinterprets it as a modern geometric cut-paper editorial
illustration with bold flat vector art and mid-century character
design — not a cartoonization, trace or full recreation. It keeps one
primary subject plus at most one or two meaningful supporting elements,
removes unnecessary backgrounds and details, and rebuilds the scene from
large bold flat geometric shapes, irregular polygons, curves and
slightly imperfect hand-cut silhouettes on a uniform warm cream/ivory
paper background, the illustration isolated at roughly a third to
two-thirds of the panel's width with generous negative space. Faces are
simplified to minimal geometric details with visible eyes; anatomy,
gestures, pose and key accessories stay recognizable; no chibi
proportions or generic cartoon styling. Color: 4–7 representative colors
from the photo in a vibrant retro palette with strong warm–cool
contrast — saturated clean color blocks, no realistic shading,
gradients or heavy black outlines. Texture is extremely subtle: fine
stipple grain, halftone dots, delicate Risograph ink texture. Output is
one complete poster per photo with no text, borders, labels, logos or
watermarks. Excluded: photorealistic illustration, 3D, anime,
watercolor, automatic vector tracing, complex scenes, thick outlines,
realistic shadows, excessive grain, unnecessary props.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's first comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents prompts that turn photos
into high-saturation paper-cut images; they suit people and landscapes.
The top half keeps the original photo; the bottom half filters out
unnecessary background, keeps the core visual focus, and rebuilds the
picture from large shapes of colored paper in bright, high-saturation
colors. His favorite thing about it: it is like giving the photo a fresh
visual translation — details are simplified, but the most emotional part
becomes more vivid. Readers are invited to copy the prompt from the
images / first comment, post their results, share, and follow for the
next prompt.
