## The prompt

The reel's author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt (this
one written in Chinese). The creator publishes it for followers to copy.
The full text lives in that comment, linked above; what follows is a
structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent poster per photo, on a 3:4 canvas
in two equal halves. The top half preserves the original photograph,
subjects unchanged, with only slight brightness/color/texture cleanup
for a cleaner magazine feel; the background may be extended, never
stretched or distorted. The bottom half redraws the photo's most
memorable protagonist as a simple illustration — only the most
important contours, shapes, actions and representative features kept
(for food: the plate, main dish, main sides, key colors; for a person:
pose, clothing outline, main action; for a building: exterior, windows,
roof, most distinctive structure) — in simple color blocks, geometric
shapes and clean lines, neither realistic nor cartoon. The ground is
rice-white, cream, or a very pale color drawn from the photo, with
generous blank space and a quiet, quality feel. Colors are the photo's
most beautiful, lively colors reorganized — brightness slightly raised,
warm, fresh and comfortable, never over-saturated, fluorescent or
candy-cheap. Text is minimal: a short title, a number, a place, a date,
one small sentence, never overpowering. The reference feel is travel
magazine, independent publication, art-exhibition poster, high-quality
brand visual. A first-use tip: pick photos whose subject reads at a
glance.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) rounds up a photo treatment he liked
before: the top half keeps the original photo, and the bottom half
reorganizes only its most memorable elements into a simple, clean
illustration — like a bespoke poster or travel souvenir card for that
photo. It suits any everyday photo with one instantly recognizable
subject: a bowl of beef soup, a plate of chicken rice, a breakfast, an
old shop, a building, a cat, a tree. Readers are invited to copy the
prompt from the first comment, post their results under it, share with
friends, and follow so they don't miss the next instruction.
