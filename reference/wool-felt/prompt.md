## The prompt

The companion reel's author comment carries the creator's full GPT
prompt (written in Chinese, with a Chinese introduction). The creator
publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in that
comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The shared two-zone template applies (each photo its own independent
high-end design poster, 3:4, zones strictly 1:1; top = original photo
preserved with slight high-end grading only). The BOTTOM zone
prioritizes a picture-book minimalist freehand （極簡寫意） over a
realistic wool-felt reconstruction: first understand the photo's most
memorable subject, structure, emotion and narrative relationships,
then re-generalize them into an extremely simple, poetic picture-book
composition converted into a handmade wool-felt illustration. It
actively deletes ~60–70% of background detail, duplicate objects and
visual clutter, keeping only the key elements that carry the original's
identity and spirit; everything else is simplified into soft, symbolic
shapes — never an object-by-object copy. Composition keeps the
small-scale subject + large white space relation: the illustration
occupies only ~20–25% of the zone, centered or slightly off-center in
pure white space, never filling the frame; flat 2.5D composition; only
minimal ground/support relations; no miniature landscapes, complete 3D
scenes or realistic 3D models. Craft: all elements show real
high-grade needle-felt work — felt-fiber texture, soft fuzzy edges,
matte finish, natural handmade imperfections; subjects generalized
into rounded, soft, symbolic wool-felt shapes. Color: the photo's
brightest, freshest, most vital colors extracted and re-mixed (not an
average sampling), with overall brightness and color purity raised and
lots of pure warm white as breathing space. Text: minimal only — one
short, gentle handwritten-style line near or below the illustration.
Overall: minimalist picture-book composition + real wool-felt craft +
fresh healing colors + small-scale subject + large artistic white
space. Excluded: object-by-object conversion, realistic wool-felt
scenes, complex backgrounds, miniature-model feel, realistic 3D feel,
filled frames, grayness/haze, aged looks, heavy Morandi muting,
dark-brown filters, fluorescent colors, cheap candy or cartoon feel,
templated poster feel.

> Full prompt text: see the source reel's author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The captions (Traditional Chinese) frame it as "half real, half
wool-felt picture book." It is not lazily slapping a wool-felt style
over the whole photo: the top stays the original photo; the bottom
"combs through" the same photo again — delete about 60–70% of the
background, clutter and unimportant detail, keeping only what gives
memory points (subject, things, relationships, emotion), then remake
it as a small wool-felt picture-book illustration. The combination is
the point: real photo + picture-book composition + small-scale
wool-felt subject + lots of white space. Above is the real memory;
below is that memory redrawn as a picture-book page. The key: the
bottom must not be filled — the subject is only about 20–25%, the rest
left blank; more space makes the whole image lighter. Phone-album
photos (travel, people, pets, street scenes, food, mountains, sea,
sunsets) all suit it.
