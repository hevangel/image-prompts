## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and reel).
The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium minimalist design poster
per photo, on a 3:4 canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves
the original photograph faithfully with only subtle refined grading.
The bottom half extracts only the most recognizable subjects,
silhouettes, structures, poses and narrative relationships — no
object-by-object copying — and rebuilds them as an abstract
architectural illustration / geometric collage / playful modernism /
contemporary editorial illustration via Gestalt reduction: only the
shapes, contours, lines and symbols needed for instant recognition,
actively reorganized (possibly enlarged, cropped, staggered). There is
one clear core visual anchor, relatively small, on a generous
warm-white ground (warm white, off-white, or very pale sky blue / mint
green); it may sit off-center, touch an edge or be partly cropped,
with recomposition explicitly allowed and breathing room around it.
Colors: the photo's own colors distilled to a few bright, fresh
accents (sky blue, aqua blue-green, fresh green, warm yellow, coral,
peach pink) with clarity preserved — muddy or Morandi tones, dark
brown, gray-weakened pastels, fluorescent colors, childish candy
colors and oversaturating filters are banned. Typography is sparing: a
short title, a number, micro-annotations or archival-style words
derived from the subject, quietly placed in the white space or along
edges, with the layout discipline of premium brand design,
architecture magazines and international design studios. Excluded:
copying the whole photo, retaining clutter or complex backgrounds,
over-realistic or 3D rendering, harsh commercial design and cheap
templates.

> Full prompt text: see the source posts' author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents a prompt that makes
everyday photos re-tell their stories: every photo holds a story and
need not be spectacular — the top half keeps the photo while the
bottom reorganizes what is most worth preserving (a childhood noodle
memory, an old grocery, a market clothing stall, an overpass and gas
station, a familiar road, laundry in the afternoon sun, a small
relationship) into simple geometric shapes, lines, color blocks and
white space, so an ordinary photo gains breathing room. The set's
longer caption adds that emotion and memory, not the scene's
completeness, are what matter. Readers are invited to copy the prompt
from the images / first comment, post their finished work under it,
share, and follow.
