## The prompt

The source post's first comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it for
followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked above;
what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium minimalist poster per
photo, on a 3:4 canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the
original photograph faithfully with only subtle refined grading;
surroundings may be extended but the subject never altered. The bottom
half does not copy or trace the photo: it first interprets the photo's
essential meaning, atmosphere, symbolism and narrative, then extracts
only the most recognizable subjects, silhouettes, structures and flow,
removing most background so the scene becomes a memorable visual symbol
that stands on its own. The visual language is abstract architectural
illustration, geometric collage and playful modernism — simplified
architectural silhouettes, organic geometric shapes, flat planar color
blocks, delicate linework and subtly irregular handmade edges — with
Gestalt-style simplification, generous negative space and a single
dominant visual anchor, placed asymmetrically as the photo's flow
dictates. Color is taken from the photo's brightest, freshest, most
vibrant signature colors (never an average), raised in brightness and
purity with restraint — clear sky blue, turquoise, fresh green, warm
yellow, coral, soft peach — against abundant warm white or ivory, for
a sunny, airy, quietly premium feel. Typography is optional and
minimal: a small English title, numbering or tiny annotations resting
in the negative space. Excluded: photorealism in the lower half,
complex backgrounds, clutter, cartoon or childish design, dull or
muddy colors, Morandi palettes, vintage filters, fluorescent or candy
colors, borders, logos and watermarks.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's first comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents a set of prompts that turn
photos into bright illustrations. Lately he has been trying different
prompts on old photos he could not bear to delete, and finds the same
photo feels different each time it is deconstructed a different way:
this time the top half keeps the original photo while the bottom half
removes the complex background and recomposes what remains with simple
shapes, bright color blocks and white space. He loves this set's
colors — like sunlight coming in, bright and clean, setting the
subject off naturally. The photo itself does not change, only the way
of seeing it; the point is not always to make a photo prettier but to
discover something new in familiar scenes. Readers are invited to copy
the prompt from the images / first comment, say which photo they would
try, share their results, and follow for the next prompt.
