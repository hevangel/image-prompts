## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the post body and in the author's first comment on the set post. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives there, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns each uploaded photograph into its own independent "Original Photography × Relaxed Hand-Drawn Chibi Illustration" comparison poster on a strict vertical 3:4 canvas, split horizontally into two seamless equal 50% sections — no text, borders, labels, logos or watermarks:

- **Top half — original photography.** A faithful copy of the original photo — composition, framing, lighting, subject, details and colours — with no changes.
- **Bottom half — relaxed hand-drawn chibi.** The same subject redrawn as a tiny hand-drawn chibi character only 2–3 heads tall, with half-lidded "dead fish eyes" (死魚眼), short limbs, and a bored "I don't want to go to work today" expression. It keeps the person's distinctive features and pose, but adds a goofy, dazed charm. The character is drawn small, surrounded by 60–75% clean near-white negative space — like a little creature quietly staying in its own world.
- **Mood.** Small, lonely, effortlessly charming, and quietly funny. No big sparkling eyes, no radiant smile, no forced cuteness — the lazy, can't-be-bothered feeling is the point. The prompt's own closing line: "The final artwork should feel small, lonely, effortlessly charming, and quietly funny."
- **Tip in the caption.** If the character comes out too lively, ask the AI to make the eyes lazier and the figure smaller.
- **Scope.** Works for selfies and everyday life photos, people and animals; the examples cover people, a cat, a corgi and a capybara.
