## The prompt

The source posts' first comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction formatted as numbered items).
The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one premium editorial comparison poster per photo —
uniquely in 9:16 — split into exactly equal top and bottom halves. The
top half preserves the photo faithfully with only subtle editorial
color grading; the subject is never distorted. The bottom half
reinterprets the most recognizable subjects, silhouettes, poses and
structures as a bold experimental CMYK halftone editorial poster:
simplify and artistically reorganize the essential elements rather than
filtering or redrawing the whole scene, keeping clear visual
recognition. Subject and background are built from visible, regularly
arranged circular CMYK halftone dots, angled screening rows, authentic
print rosettes and subtle cyan/magenta registration offsets, with
near-black shadows and cream highlights for strong contrast. Color
fields run vibrant hot yellow → orange → red — a clean, contemporary,
high-energy editorial aesthetic, not a faded vintage look. Typography
is integral: an oversized milky-white bold sans-serif title with a soft
blurred glow, arranged around the subject's silhouette, plus minimal
sharp black supporting copy and one tiny metadata line. Excluded: 3D
mockups, logos, watermarks, random grain, scratches, distressed
textures, unnecessary text and cheap retro effects.

> Full prompt text: see the source posts' first comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents a prompt that processes
photos in a retro film style with a bit of an old-magazine-cover feel.
The top half keeps the photo, only slightly tidying its color and
light; the bottom half switches rendering — like a magazine cover
magnified, you see the regular little halftone dots one by one, and the
colors do not go faded-retro but use bright yellow, orange and red for
stronger visual impact. His favorite thing: the treatment gives the
photo a new name — what was just a moment from everyday life becomes,
once titled, very much like a collectible artwork. Readers are invited
to copy the prompt from the images / first comment, share it with
friends, and follow for the next prompt.
