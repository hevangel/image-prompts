## The prompt

The source post's pinned comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it for
followers to copy. The full text lives in that pinned comment, linked
above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per uploaded photo —
never a collage — on a 3:4 canvas split into two equal halves. The top
half preserves the original photograph faithfully, with only subtle
editorial grading, and surroundings may be extended but the subject
never altered.

The bottom half is an extraction and reconstruction, not a repaint of
the whole scene: find the photo's 2–5 most important elements, choose
the single strongest subject or silhouette as the focus, and remove or
drastically simplify 70–90% of backgrounds, clutter and minor detail.
The recomposed scene keeps roughly half to three-quarters of its area
visually quiet — clean white or warm off-white — with secondary
elements reduced to a few suggestive shapes and color fields. The
medium is expressive minimal impasto oil painting: thick sculptural
brushstrokes, heavily layered pigment, palette-knife texture, visible
paint ridges and tactile buildup, applied most thickly to the most
important forms and left loose or untouched elsewhere — never uniform
texture everywhere. Color comes from the photo's own dominant colors,
intensified into rich, vivid, saturated accents; muddy, dusty, pastel
or low-saturation schemes are rejected, and neutrals only support.
Uniquely among these prompts, this one allows an optional short
English title (1–4 words) with at most one short supporting phrase,
set small in the negative space. Its stated priority is subject, then
emotion, gesture, recognizable memory, structure, color, texture, and
only then typography. Excluded: full-scene repainting, crowded
backgrounds, photorealistic painting, smooth digital rendering, large
text blocks, borders, logos and watermarks.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's pinned comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents a set of oil-painting
instructions with real thickness, unlike ordinary photo-to-oil
conversions: the top half keeps the original photo, and the bottom
half first tidies the composition — dropping the complex background,
keeping the memorable focal points — then redraws them in very thick
oil paint, in places built up layer on layer like cream, with visible
pigment ridges and brushstroke relief. Colors grow more vivid, and
the deliberate white space keeps subject and color prominent without
the painting ever feeling crowded. Readers are invited to copy the
prompt from the images / first comment, comment with the photo they'd
try, share, and follow for the next prompt.
