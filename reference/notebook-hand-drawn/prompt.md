## The prompt

The source post's pinned first comment carries the creator's full GPT
prompt (in English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes
it for followers to copy: paste the prompt into an image model, upload
your own photo, and share the result. The full text lives in that pinned
comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent poster per uploaded photo — never a
collage — on a 3:4 portrait canvas split horizontally into two exactly
equal halves, with no borders, dividing lines, labels, text, logos or
watermarks. The top half preserves the original photograph faithfully —
subject, composition, pose, lighting and color atmosphere — with only
extremely subtle editorial grading, and surroundings may be extended
naturally but never stretched, repainted or cropped away.

The bottom half is a complete reinterpretation: 100% illustration, 0%
photography, in a delicate botanical-ink style drawn with a fine black
fineliner and controlled parallel and cross-hatching. It first picks out
the photo's most recognizable subjects and relationships, keeps their
defining features in restrained linework, and removes clutter — some
areas intricate, others minimal or entirely blank for deliberate
negative space. Color is strictly rationed: two dominant colors
extracted from the photo plus one derived complementary accent, black
ink, and warm-white paper — nothing else. The color goes on as large,
flat, irregular abstract blocks (broad-marker organic shapes) laid
behind and around the subject as an independent layer; blocks may cross
the ink lines or disappear behind the subject, with intentional
imperfections and a few small dots for rhythm, like a handmade
screen-print or sketchbook page. The background is dramatically
simplified — a few essential contours plus generous warm-white negative
space — on heavyweight warm-white notebook paper with subtle fibers and
grain. Explicitly excluded: photorealism, digital-painting and 3D looks,
smooth gradients, watercolor bleeding, colored outlines, busy
backgrounds, full-coverage coloring, perfect geometry, generic floral
decoration, arbitrary colors, and dense all-over linework.

> Full prompt text: see the source post's pinned first comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents the prompt set as a way to
turn photos into a notebook hand-drawn look: the top half keeps the
original photo, while the bottom half redraws people, buildings and
objects with very fine fineliner strokes — extra fine lines only where
shape and shadow need clarity, and large areas left as blank white
paper. Its favorite part is the color: a few of the photo's most
characteristic colors, reworked into irregular blocks that don't follow
the outlines — sometimes spilling outside the lines, sometimes hiding
behind the subject — like pen outlines casually colored in with crayon
or colored pencil: slightly messy, but full of hand-drawn charm, and
the whitespace keeps the image light and airy. It invites readers to
copy the prompt from the images / first comment, try it on a photo of
their own, comment with what they'd try, and share and follow for the
next prompt. Photo source: Pexels.
