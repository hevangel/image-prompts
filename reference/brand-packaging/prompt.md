## The prompt

The author comments on SET ZH (Chinese) and the companion video
(English) carry the creator's full GPT prompt — parallel versions of
one prompt. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full
text lives in those comments, linked above; what follows is a
structural summary.

Each uploaded photo becomes its own independent premium design poster,
output separately, no collages; vertical 3:4 with two zones of exactly
50% each. The TOP zone preserves the original photo — subject identity,
structure, proportions, pose, real texture, natural light and original
color mood — with only subtle high-end editorial grading; sky, ground
and surroundings may extend naturally; the subject is never stretched
or redesigned. The BOTTOM zone analyzes the photo's most recognizable
subject, silhouette, pose, structure, colors, materials, mood and story
and rebuilds them as a complete brand packaging and visual-identity
system — traits abstracted into line illustrations, symbols, motifs,
patterns and color blocks, never the photo printed directly on every
package. The system auto-selects 5–8 carriers suited to the theme
(paper bag, box, gift box, envelope, label, hang tag, ticket, card,
sticker, wrapping paper, booklet), with 1–2 hero packages and the rest
arranged as a modular studio presentation board with aligned edges,
scale contrast and generous white space. Palette: soft, airy, slightly
desaturated — powder/mist/sky blues as the base with ivory, cream,
light beige, soft gray-green and architectural neutrals, plus a little
dusty rose as accent; carriers may rebalance proportions but stay in
one color system. Materials: realistic premium matte/textured paper
and card with embossing, die-cutting and folding under soft natural
light; no plastic feel, harsh reflections or exaggerated 3D.
Typography: a short English brand title derived from the photo plus a
few sub-lines, numbers or micro-captions, placed differently per
carrier (vertical, along edges, around seals, across box faces), never
the same title layout repeated. Exclusions: fixed product combos,
repeated templates, ordinary brand mockups, e-commerce display feel,
pasting the original photo onto all products, cheap plastic feel,
glossy glowing 3D renders, packages that all look identical, random
mockup piles.

## English translation of the introduction

The captions say: instead of merely restyling a photo, develop it into
a complete brand packaging system — the top half keeps the original
photo while the bottom half distills the photo's most distinctive
elements (person, scenery, silhouette, movement, colors, mood) into
paper bags, boxes, hang tags, stickers, cards, labels and wrapping
paper that all read as one brand. The photo's traits must first be
understood and turned into graphics, colors, typography and patterns
rather than copy-pasted onto packages. Any subject works — portraits,
landscapes, food, cars, plants, animals.
