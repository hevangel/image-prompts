## The prompt

The source posts' first comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it
for followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked
above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the original
photograph faithfully with only extremely subtle grading. The bottom
half — named "Visual Memory Extraction × Minimal Jacquard Tapestry" —
is not a trace, a full redraw or a mechanical textile filter. It first
extracts the photo's 2–5 strongest visual memory points (main subject,
recognizable contours, poses, relationships, a few key objects,
structure, light), removes roughly 70–90% of background, decoration
and secondary information, and rearranges what remains into a cleaner
composition with clear hierarchy and real negative space — where the
quiet areas belong to the woven composition itself (large simplified
low-detail woven fields), never an external frame or fabric margin.
Every retained element is genuinely reconstructed through jacquard
tapestry logic: visible warp and weft, interlaced yarn texture, woven
color blocks, thread-level irregularity and natural stair-stepping
along curves — no smooth photographic detail surviving under a fabric
overlay. The palette is about 6–8 yarn colors extracted from the photo,
refined and unified. Excluded: studio backgrounds, fabric borders,
selvedges, loose threads, textile-sample presentation, pixel art,
digital mosaic, smooth gradients, paper texture, hand-drawn lines, and
desaturated or muddy palettes.

> Full prompt text: see the source posts' first comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents a prompt that re-"weaves" a
photo. It works especially well for photos with clear outlines,
distinct colors, or clearly defined poses and movement. The method
keeps the photo's structural contours and its most important small
patch of light and color, then re-weaves the image thread by thread:
people, clothing, sky, architecture, light and shadow lose their
smooth photographic detail and become warp and weft threads of
different colors interlacing into new contours and color blocks.
Because much is removed, you more easily see the part of the photo
worth remembering — and this time the image is deliberately not filled
edge to edge, keeping some space, so it does not look like a typical
full-coverage carpet pattern. Readers are invited to copy the prompt
from the images / first comment, comment with the photo they would
most like to try, share, and follow for the next prompt.
