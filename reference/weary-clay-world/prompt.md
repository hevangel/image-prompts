## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the author's pinned comment, in English, under the style title "Original Photography × Handmade Clay Little World — Deadpan Chibi Edition". The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives there, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns each uploaded photograph independently into a premium 3:4 vertical poster split horizontally into two equal 50% halves, with no borders or extra panels:

- **Top half — original photography.** A faithful copy of the original photo — composition, people, poses, expressions, outfits and setting — with a slight warm grade and sharp focus. Add nothing, remove nothing, put no text there.
- **Bottom half — handmade clay diorama.** Reinterpret the same scene as a miniature handmade polymer-clay diorama on a warm off-white background, with soft studio lighting, natural shadows, generous negative space (about 60–70%), a small centred clay scene and a clay ground/shadow, keeping roughly a 70/30 balance between scene and empty space.
- **Colour.** Pull 3–4 muted signature colours from the original photo and use them consistently in the clay figure and setting; the overall palette is muted, warm and low-saturation.
- **Figure style.** Deadpan chibi — small body, oversized round head, short limbs, matte polymer-clay skin with slight texture, and half-lidded "fish" eyes made from white clay discs with tiny black pupils, uneven heavy eyelids and a flat, crooked, deadpan expression. Preserve each person's distinctive features, clothing, hairstyle and props, simplify props, add only one or two tiny clay props, and introduce no new subjects.
- **Text.** Add an original 8–16-character Traditional Chinese micro-poem in 2–3 short handwritten pencil-style lines in the clay half, matching the mood of the scene.
- **Forbidden.** No glossy plastic or CGI look, no anime eyes, no crowded backgrounds, no English text, logos or watermarks, and no extra panels, duplicate figures or added text.

> Full prompt text: see the author's pinned comment, linked above.

## English translation of the introduction

The post's caption, translated: "If you knead everyday life into clay, even world-weariness becomes a little bit cute. [The Weary Handmade Clay Miniature World] This time I kneaded everyday photos into a group of little clay figures who are world-weary but so cute: two people eating popcorn at the movies, a woman tidying her mood while she cooks, a grandmother holding a cat on a cold day, a child standing in a puddle, a man polishing his car, a father and son who caught a big fish… Each one is a scene we have all lived through — only, turned into clay, even feeling tired becomes soft. Tell me in the comments: which one looks most like your life? Or try it with your own photo — the prompt is in the comments. You are also welcome to post your work back here to share with everyone."
