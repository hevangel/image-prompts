## The prompt

The full prompt sits in the post caption and identically in the author's first comment on the set post. The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives there, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt turns each uploaded photograph, separately and never merged, into a premium "Photography × Paper Folk Story" comparison poster, in a blend of Handmade Paper Cut-Out, Layered Paper Collage, Minimal Folk Illustration and Negative Space Editorial Design, on a strict 3:4 portrait canvas split horizontally into two exactly equal halves (50/50), with no borders, text, labels, logos or watermarks:

- **Layout.** Top half: a faithful copy of the original photo — same subjects, faces, expressions, poses, clothing, proportions, architecture, objects, lighting, textures and colors, with only subtle editorial color grading and minimal cropping or natural background extension.
- **Bottom half — transformation.** The 3–5 most distinctive visual elements rebuilt as a small, refined paper-cut composition that stays recognizable (same silhouettes, poses, proportions, gestures, spatial relationships and mood), with unnecessary details removed. Never invent or add anything not in the source — no extra people, animals, objects, buildings, accessories, flowers, decoration or imaginary scenery; simplify rather than add.
- **Negative space.** About 70% of the bottom half stays empty — clean white or subtly off-white paper with a faint paper texture — while the artwork takes only about 25–35% of the bottom half, placed centered, slightly low, or asymmetric depending on the subject; the background is never filled with extra scenery or decoration.
- **Paper-craft look.** Layered matte colored paper with visible fibers, uneven pigmentation and delicate wrinkles, slightly irregular hand-cut edges, minimal overlapping shapes and soft realistic shadows between layers; a charming handcrafted children's storybook feel with a gentle stop-motion quality. Avoid excessive detail, glossy surfaces, clay, plastic, 3D figurines, photorealistic rendering and smooth vector graphics.
- **Color.** Pull 2–4 representative colors directly from each original photo, keeping its color relationships and mood; never force a fixed warm or vintage palette.
- **Output/behavior.** One premium 3:4 vertical diptych per uploaded photo — original on top, paper-cut below, about 70% clean negative space — preserving the original story, cutting unnecessary detail, introducing nothing absent from the source, and generating directly without asking questions.

## English translation of the introduction

You don't need scissors or colored paper to turn a photo into a cute handmade paper-cut style ✂️ Hey, this photo transformation is kind of cute 😊 It's not oil painting, and it's not cartoon — it's like someone took colored paper and cut out the people and scenery from the photo, piece by piece, then pasted them on. It reminds me of art class as a kid, with scissors, colored paper and glue, cutting people, houses or little animals into different shapes, then cutting, gluing and pasting them into a picture — and the glue hadn't dried, so the whole picture was still wet 😅 This time I wanted AI to make that handmade paper-cut feeling! The top half is the original photo, and the bottom half turns the most distinctive parts of the photo into simple, cute paper-cut shapes. You can still see the feeling of paper layered up, edges a little uneven, with faint shadows, just like it was really cut by hand. And there's a little detail I love this time! The bottom half isn't stuffed full — it looks clean, with a storybook feel, so when you look at the paper-cut picture you can still recognize the familiar people and scenery from the original photo ☺️ As a kid we used scissors and colored paper for crafts; grown up, we use this prompt to cut-and-paste our memories back. (The caption closes with the usual notes: the full GPT prompt is in the first comment, free to copy and use; example photos are from Pexels.)
