## The prompt

The source posts' first comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction). The creator publishes it
for followers to copy. The full text lives in that comment, linked
above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the original
photograph faithfully, never converted to illustration. The bottom
half is a minimalist single-color line-art poster on a pure black
background with an authentic handmade, imperfect print feel — dry
brush, worn broad-tip markers, distressed screen printing, underground
street prints. Its color rule is strict: analyze the photo and select
exactly one recognizable signature color — a distinctive chromatic hue,
not the largest-area color and not a neutral; if the photo is very low
saturation, take its clearest subtle hue with at most a minimal lift.
The lower half then uses only black, white typography, and that one
accent color — no second accent, no gradients, no tonal variations.
One dominant subject is kept and 70–90% of background and secondary
detail eliminated, but the subject is never deformed: realistic
proportions and physically plausible structure are mandatory, and all
reinterpretation comes from simplifying contours, enlarging, cropping
and recomposing. The subject is drawn large (about 65–80% of the lower
half) as a hollow contour in the accent color, mostly black inside,
with authentically irregular dry-marker strokes — variable width and
pressure, feathered edges, broken pigment, skipped strokes, slight
bleeding. Typography: a bold condensed white uppercase title of 1–3
words at upper left, an optional small vertical micro-caption along
the right edge, and only 2–3 subtle handmade accent marks near the
edges. Its closing principle, in paraphrase: the photograph chooses
the color; the brush strokes reinterpret the photograph. Excluded:
default red, clean vector outlines, uniform stroke widths, smooth
digital curves, realistic shadows, 3D, cute cartoons, surreal
mutations, logos and commercial advertising aesthetics.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents the "rough hand-smeared
lines" prompt. It suits images with a simple subject and simple colors
that emphasize outlines — close-ups, for example. Unlike clean, slick
or tech-feeling lines, it keeps a little imperfection, like a dried-out
or nearly empty old marker, or the smear marks left by a hand, with
uneven thickness and edges that are not clean — so although the result
looks simple, it never feels too digital or too smooth. The worn lines
redraw the photo's subject; he likes that it does not pursue
completeness — it has a handmade street-notice feel, looks simple but
has real character, and the same photo takes on a completely different
temperament in this line language. Readers are invited to copy the
prompt from the images / first comment, share their results, and
follow for the next prompt.
