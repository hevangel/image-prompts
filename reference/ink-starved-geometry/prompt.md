## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and reel).
The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the photograph
faithfully with only subtle high-end grading. The bottom half extracts
the most recognizable subject, silhouette, posture and narrative
relationship, understands its core meaning, cultural attributes or
emotional relationship first, and reconstructs it as a graphic
composition combining folk-culture catalog, antique rubbing print and
modern graphic design: ONE core visual motif plus very few narrative
auxiliary forms — no one-by-one translation of elements, no generic
traditional patterns — using silhouette, negative space, partial
enlargement, shape merging and symbolic transformation. Its geometry is
a rigorous framework derived from the subject's own structure (circles,
squares, triangles, arcs, axes, repeated proportions) as the matrix;
all forms, typography and negative space align via shared tangencies,
common axes, nesting, mirroring, proportional progression or
edge-cropping, obeying one structural logic with a clear center of
gravity, hierarchy and rhythm — no random scatter or decorative
accumulation. Color: the photo's most distinctive, lively colors
reorganized into a clean restrained paper-print palette — a bright
light paper ground, one dark color as the skeleton, one or two theme
colors from the photo — never muddy fake aging, stereotyped folk
palettes or chaotic multicolor. Texture is a hybrid of woodblock
rubbing, dry-brush screen print and vintage catalog materiality:
broken ink （缺墨）, abrasion, exposed paper and uneven ink density
mainly INSIDE the graphic forms, while geometric boundaries, key
contours and the layout stay crisp — rough material against rigorous
order. Typography is supporting only: a small title and
micro-annotations following geometric axes and negative space, never
competing for the center. Excluded: collage-like assembly, arbitrary
traditional symbols, evenly distributed layouts, realistic illustration
and template design.

> Full prompt text: see the source posts' author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) frames the style as a decluttering
（斷捨離） treatment. When a photo holds many things it is hard to know
what to remove, since some are picture, some memory, some the emotion
of the moment — so the AI tidies the photo: the top half keeps the
original photo as unaltered as possible, while the bottom half keeps
only the most memorable people and objects, reorganized into simple
shapes, color blocks and white space, finished with an old-print feel —
some areas not fully inked, some worn, some showing the paper through.
The point is not the resulting "style" but that with fewer things, the
photo's emotion and atmosphere come through more clearly. His worked
example: for a street-vendor photo, what carries the value is the
conical hat, the elderly woman's seated posture, the few buckets of
flowers and the bicycle; signs, clutter and passers-by are wiped away,
colors are taken from the original photo (never re-matched arbitrarily),
and the worn-print finish is added. Readers are invited to copy the
prompt from the first comment, post their results under it, share, and
follow.
