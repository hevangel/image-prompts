## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and reel).
The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one standalone high-end poster per photo, on a 3:4
canvas in two equal halves. The top half retains the photograph with
only subtle sophisticated grading. The bottom half reinterprets the
most recognizable subject, silhouette, pose and narrative relationship
as a surreal, minimalist printmaking composition: a restrained visual
anchor at small scale in vast negative space, where the photo's
movement, direction, light/shadow or spatial relationship becomes ONE
dynamic field — falling, rising, flowing, radiating, or rippling —
built from fine lines, broken trajectories, rough halftone and
grain-density variation, densest at the core and dissolving into blank
space; effects are never stacked. Color is strict: ONE signature color
extracted from the photo, plus warm white / ivory / natural paper, plus
a little black — two to three colors total — with tonal variation
achieved through halftone density, ink layering and exposed paper, not
gradients. Texture is screen-print / Risograph tactility: coarse grain,
paper fibers, uneven ink, slight misregistration; no CGI, 3D or smooth
digital look. Typography is restrained experimental art-book
micro-typography: an extremely short English title from the photo's
action, distance, state, mood or metaphor, plus 2–4 micro-text groups
(phrases, status terms, location/object info, serial numbers,
coordinate-like numerals, directional words, archival labels,
observational notes) placed along horizons, motion trajectories, the
subject's axis or the edges of negative space. Excluded: complex
scenes, multiple focal points, excess decoration, ordinary gradients,
commercial or template-like design.

> Full prompt text: see the source posts' author comment (linked above).

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents the effect as giving the
image a coarse grain, as if ground with sandpaper. The top half keeps
the photo; the bottom half strips background, noise and excess detail,
keeps the memorable elements — an everyday moment, a night platform,
soldiers at dusk, crossing tracks, a traffic light — and rebuilds them
as coarse sandpaper grain plus broken lines, denser near the subject
and fragmenting outward into large white space, preserving a sense of
movement, direction, light, distance, or the feeling of the instant.
Readers are invited to copy the prompt from the images / first
comment, post their results in the comments, share, and follow.
