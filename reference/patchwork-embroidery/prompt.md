## The prompt

The source posts' author comment carries the creator's full GPT prompt
(in English, with a Chinese introduction; identical on set and reel).
The creator publishes it for followers to copy. The full text lives in
that comment, linked above; what follows is a structural summary.

The prompt asks for one independent premium design poster per photo,
on a 3:4 canvas in two equal halves. The top half preserves the
original photograph faithfully with only subtle refined grading. The
bottom half extracts only the most recognizable contours, pose,
hairstyle, clothing, props and narrative relationships and rebuilds
them as a stitched embroidery collage — patchwork construction,
appliqué embroidery, hand-stitched poster — that unmistakably looks
made of fabric and thread, never a refined illustration or digital
painting: large fabric patches with bold visible stitching, enlarged
backstitch / chain stitch / running stitch / cross-stitch language,
stitches thick, sparse and legible from a distance; frayed edges,
exposed loose threads, uneven cloth borders, stitched seams; a few
buttons, miniature fabric labels or stitched tags allowed if they
never compete with the subject. There is generous negative space on a
warm white / ivory / cream / pale linen ground, with the subject
relatively small and possibly off-center, edge-touching or partly
cropped. The palette is limited to 2–3 main colors plus dark
gray/black stitching and the warm neutral fabric ground; excess photo
colors are discarded; low saturation; warm and handmade. Small
fabric-label typography, dates, numbers or brief handwritten phrases
are allowed subtly. The mood is rustic, handmade, vintage, comforting,
quiet, collectible. Excluded: refined illustration, detailed digital
painting, storybook style, smooth gradients, polished vector,
excessive lace or detail, invisible stitches or fabric texture, thin
stitching, weak embroidery, complete scene reproduction, full-frame
composition, an oversized subject, saturated multicolor, 3D,
commercial template, AI plastic texture.

## English translation of the introduction

The caption (Traditional Chinese) presents re-sewing a photo with
cloth and thread: the top half keeps the original photo; the bottom
half is not a copy-embroidery — excess background, unimportant objects
and complex colors are deleted first, keeping the subject's contours,
action and the scene's feeling, then rebuilt from fabric patches
joined by thick, obvious stitching with uneven edges, loose threads
and fraying, imperfect the way real handwork is. It stays recognizable
as the same photo from a distance, but quieter, with lots of blank
space and a vintage collectible feel. Readers are invited to copy the
prompt from the images / first comment, try it with their own photos,
and upload their reworked images to share.
